# BIN Lore Wiki

**BIN Lore** is this site: a fan-made archive of *Barely Informed News*, created and maintained by [[teflon-ted|Teflon Ted]] and launched in September 2026. It didn't take long for the broadcast to notice it. This page records each time the show has talked about the wiki on air.

## The 2026-10-10 Review

The first on-air read-through was the most thorough:

- Reading the home page's description of the "premier satirical broadcast network": "That's what we're doing?" "Not intentionally now, but it's the lane we ended up in."
- Liliana's page described her long absence as a self-granted sabbatical, complete with a travel slideshow. Nobody could remember the slideshow, but: "I can't refute any of this because this is more accurate than my own memory."
- The wiki got the network's blessing: "I also do approve of this having fully made up things in it, for the record, so have at it."
- At the time, auto-created pages still said they were made "after appearing in three extracted episodes." The hosts caught it: "Threshold three? I'm sensing the power of the algorithm here," after an earlier "I'm sensing a little bit of AI vibes in here."
- Kendelle's page didn't have a picture yet, and it was out of date: it called her Case's girlfriend. "Fiance now, actually."
- The hosts kept checking whether Chris's sub-100 credit score was on there. It wasn't.
- Crackhead Yogi Bear was mentioned but didn't have his own page: "I'm sorry that Crackhead Yogi Bear is not in their propaganda."
- "I like the idea of someone who's never seen any of this just stumbling somehow across this wiki and being like, what?"
