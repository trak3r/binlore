# Rock Ice

**Rock Ice** is a prior beverage sponsorship cited by [[../characters/cryptozeus|Brandon (CryptoZeu$)]] on *Barely Informed News* — a Kid Rock–branded drink he described as **over 30% water** and sold at Kroger.

## Overview & Brand Lore

Unlike active CryptoZeu$ underwriters such as [[gooters|Gooters]] or [[milk-cola|Milk Cola]], Rock Ice appears in the archive as a **past** sponsorship Brandon references while on air, not as an ongoing live read. The product's defining on-air attribute is its watery composition.

- **[[../episodes/2026-02-10|2026-02-10]]:** Brandon references a prior Kid Rock "Rock Ice" sponsorship (over 30% water, sold at Kroger) ([1:33:04]).
