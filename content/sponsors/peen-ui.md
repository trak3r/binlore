# Peen.UI

![The Peen.ui landing page entered into evidence at Crum's trial](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/peen-ui.jpg)

**Peen.UI** (written **PNUI** in early coverage, styled **Peen.ui** on its own landing page) is an AI startup that photographs your urine and turns its color into stock advice. Its tagline: *"Stock advice, straight from the source."* It entered the broadcast as [[../characters/crum|Crum]]'s sponsor, sued him within the same breath, took him to trial on [[../episodes/2026-03-07|2026-03-07]], and won the verdict that sent Crum to the gorilla in the [[../storylines/crum-dick-punch|Crum Dick Punch]]. Crum also claims to have co-founded it.

## The Product

Crum's first pitch ([[../episodes/2026-02-10|2026-02-10]] [1:21:41]) is the canonical spec: *"you hold your phone up to your toilet and it will use AI to read the color of your urine. And then it'll tell you basically, it'll give you stock advice based on your urine color."* He explains it's built from *"hugging nuts and comfy flop"* plus some *"low-leaves"* you install, which sounds a lot like Hugging Face, ComfyUI, and LoRAs.

Later refinements:

- **Hue and viscosity.** It *"takes the full visual gamut in and offers you up to the minute stock advice,"* and smell is on the roadmap *"if they ever break the technology down"* ([[../episodes/2026-02-28|2026-02-28]] [30:53]).
- **Brown is out.** *"If it's yellow, let it mellow. If it's brown, well then legally we can't offer stock advice for it"* ([[../episodes/2026-02-12|2026-02-12]] [11:25]).
- **Not live.** Neither Peen.UI nor its spinoff is actually up yet; both are mock-ups ([[../episodes/2026-02-28|2026-02-28]] [33:48]).
- **The landing page** shown at trial ([[../episodes/2026-03-07|2026-03-07]] [32:24]): a phone of urine-color swatches next to a stock chart, and a *"Get Started with Your Sample"* button.

## The Crum Empire

Crum's relationship to the company shifts depending on what he's being asked. He has been its sponsored talent, its investor (*"an unfortunate AI investment that went a little shower,"* [[../episodes/2026-02-17|2026-02-17]] [29:40]), and finally *"technically one of the founders of peen.ui"* alongside CEO **Lenny Riz**, which the plaintiffs say is short for **Lemur Rizler** ([[../episodes/2026-03-07|2026-03-07]] [1:50:35]). By [[../episodes/2026-03-19|2026-03-19]] [58:34] he describes his likeness as *"only been sold to peen.ui and peenucrice.ai. Both companies I'm a founder of."*

Spinoffs and fronts:

- **PNUChrist.ai** (also peenuchrist.ai, P-NU Christ.ai). Crum's rival venture, which he admits might have been made by hiring a programmer to use his *"full back end access"* to Peen.UI's model, on the theory that *"most AIs were open source"* ([[../episodes/2026-02-10|2026-02-10]] [1:24:32]). It takes your biometric data and *"offers you prayers directly to god"* ([[../episodes/2026-02-28|2026-02-28]] [31:14]), or spiritual advice based on your urine-based stock advice ([[../episodes/2026-02-14|2026-02-14]] [1:35:56]). It reportedly received a **$6 billion valuation** before launch. *"Everybody's taken a piss and thought I need to pray to god right now."* It also, through *"a couple crossed wires,"* stole about a million medical and financial records from deceased Kentucky grandmas ([[../episodes/2026-02-14|2026-02-14]] [1:35:05]). Its monthly subscription is promised to banish your demons ([[../episodes/2026-03-03|2026-03-03]] [2:01:16]).
- **PnuCrimes.ai.** The *"illegal dark website"* revealed in the title of Crum's Davos speech: *"I spent three months AI vibe coding a pee pee site to use it as a front for my illegal dark website PnuCrimes.ai for crimes."* Crum calls the speech *"financial satire… next level economic irony"* ([[../episodes/2026-03-07|2026-03-07]] [2:11:37]).

### The Trial (2026-03-07)

"A Night of Courts" ran as a full mock trial on the [[../segments/news|News]] desk, with [[../characters/case-blackwell|Case]] presiding as a newly licensed federal judge, defense attorney Cleetance Mudbutter (persistently called Mudbottom), and prosecutor Rock Justice. Chat served as the jury. Witnesses included [[../characters/hate-sock|Hate Sock]], [[../characters/don-riggles|Don Riggles]], [[../characters/coach-mcgurk|Coach McGurk]], and [[../characters/jizzbo|Jizzbo]].

- **The charges, per the judge:** *"infringing on their IP and for, um, I think just like annoying the shit out of them. Which you can be sued for actually"* [26:22].
- **Defense theories:** Crum *"was not aware the quality of his hand game was directly tied to the financial well-being of peen.ui"* [26:45]; Crum *"doesn't own a computer after his divorce. How can he be a mastermind of an online, peen-based empire?"* [32:16]; and Lenny Riz *"has no peen. Therefore, he can't run a company called peen.ui"* [1:31:24].
- **Evidence:** the landing page [32:24]; a photo of Crum with a **Peen.UI briefcase paying a gorilla** [47:55]; new Epstein files showing Crum asking Epstein for money for Peen.UI [48:51]; a question about funneling profits from extorting JAS Japanese Elevator Service into PeenU Christ [1:48:07]; and the Davos speech [2:11:37].
- **No-show plaintiff:** CEO Lenny Riz never appeared. Crum, after the verdict: *"My boy Lenny Riz never even showed up"* [2:30:06].
- **Verdict:** the jury voted *"genital slit."* Case sentenced Crum to debate a gorilla and then take a gorilla punch within weeks, plus **$6 million owed to Peen.UI** [2:23:09].

That sentence is the legal origin of the gorilla groin punch. Three days later Crum was reminded at the news desk that *"you're getting punched by the gorilla in the dick this time for your peen UI crimes"* ([[../episodes/2026-03-10|2026-03-10]] [25:35]). The punch finally landed on [[../episodes/2026-09-07|2026-09-07]]; see [[../storylines/crum-dick-punch|Crum Dick Punch]].

- **Brandon.** Asked during [[../segments/cryptozeus|CryptoZeu$]] about *"the company peen ai and its embattled founder crumb,"* [[../characters/cryptozeus|Brandon]] clarifies he was sponsored by Peen.UI, not Peen AI (*"a different company"*): *"they're pretty easy to work with, like I basically had to mention them twice,"* for *"at least three figures"* ([[../episodes/2026-02-17|2026-02-17]] [3:05:29]).
- **The network contract.** On [[../episodes/2026-08-14|2026-08-14]], [[../characters/pepito|Pepito]]'s pre-show loop urges viewers to *"Subscribe to the PNUI program, it's not a scam"* [0:14]. Case later explains that Pepito accidentally opted the audience in to PNUI data scraping, which *"was built into the contract I signed with Crum. I don't pay him, but he gets to train PNUI on the streams data."* Not the stream itself, he clarifies: *"We've got AI cameras set up in all of our toilets here"* [11:56].
