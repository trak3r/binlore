# Markinson's Wet Ham Emporium

**Markinson's Wet Ham Emporium** is a deli-front hardware sponsor in *Barely Informed News* lore — the claimed source of [[../characters/cryptozeus|Brandon's (CryptoZeu$)]] gaming PC, emphatically **not** Dell.

## Overview & Brand Lore

On air, Brandon attributes his computer to Markinson's Wet Ham Emporium as a deli sponsorship arrangement. The brand fuses wet-cured pork retail with consumer electronics underwriting, fitting the same moist-culinary sponsorship ecosystem that surrounds the CryptoZeu$ desk (see also [[gooters|Gooters]]).
