# Milk Cola

**Milk Cola** (legally styled **MLK Cola** in on-air reads) is a calcium-flavored beverage sponsor tied to [[../characters/cryptozeus|Brandon's (CryptoZeu$)]] remote gaming dispatches on *Barely Informed News*.

## Overview & Brand Lore

Network lore frames Milk Cola as a soft drink that tastes of calcium yet remains **legally distinct from milk** — a trademark-adjacent dodge that lets the brand occupy the dairy aisle without calling itself dairy. Brandon has described himself as "currently sponsored" by the product during live playthroughs.

## Sponsorship History

- **[[../episodes/2026-03-27|2026-03-27]]:** Underwrites Brandon's inverted-controls *Pepsi Man* speedrun on the CryptoZeu$ desk; Brandon confirms the Milk Cola / MLK Cola sponsorship mid-broadcast ([01:25:02]).
