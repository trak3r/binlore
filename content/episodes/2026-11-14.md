## Overview

Barely Informed News turned its Saturday broadcast over to the reconstruction of Rennerton, where ousted mayor Crum staged a press conference on behalf of Mayor Melody Von Torture and launched a jet-ski building program. Chat then elected Executive Producer Pepito the dog as mayor, and he promptly commandeered Crum's repeatedly-deceased body to raise a strip club and set the wilderness ablaze. The crew closed the session by felling a giant forest boss before signing off.
