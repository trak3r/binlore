## Overview

On this historic Season 3 finale of *Barely Informed News*, lead anchor Case Blackwell hosts the final broadcast before departing for an extended two-week international leave in Thailand, culminating in the long-awaited climax to the [[storylines/crum-dick-punch|Crum Dick Punch]] storyline. The telecast begins with executive producer Pepito setting up Labor Day expectations, followed by a news rundown covering the Lanterns series trailer posted by the White House, Trump's Confederate memorial policy reversals, Trump's defense of power-hungry AI data centers ('have fun being poor'), the GPT-6 'Astro' model autonomously speedrunning Portal, RFK Jr.'s cooking show, Ben Hooper's UPI reports on a runaway floating island in BC and a deer stuck in a bathroom, and an archival Pros vs. Joes clip of a 13-year-old female orangutan overpowering a 363-pound champion sumo wrestler. Hype Train interrupts with a synthwave rant about the perfection of jeans and raps about girls wearing denim, before turning to subs. Gaming correspondent Brandon (CryptoZeu$) takes the remote desk for Intelligent Qube on PS1 via keyboard while fielding sponsor dispatches for Gooters' $59.99 soggy cola-dipped chicken skins. The broadcast reaches its dramatic climax during Munch & Crum's debate over 'Any Last Words?'—where Crum insists his rigorous physical conditioning with weighted logs and steel underwear will withstand the blow. As the pneumatic Rock-afire Gorilla fires the fateful punch, a cataclysmic mechanical misfire erupts into an inferno, completely vaporizing the gorilla into dust and incinerating senior debate analyst Ralph Munchcut down to a living, talking skeleton. Undeterred, the skeletal Munch embraces his bone structure, argues for Crayola over RoseArt, and demands future debates as the network signs off.
