## Overview

Anchor Case Blackwell hosts a solo 'Elden Sick Day' broadcast from the newsroom, playing the Shadow of the Erdtree DLC on its surprise release day while recovering from a Disney-acquired illness. After defeating Mohg, Lord of Blood to access the Realm of Shadow, Case explores the new territory, discovers the Scadutree Fragment progression system, and overcomes the Divine Beast Dancing Lion boss to claim its ritual headwear. The broadcast concludes with a raid to Danksauce.
