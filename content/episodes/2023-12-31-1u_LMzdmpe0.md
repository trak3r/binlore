## Overview

Barely Informed News rings in the new year with the 2023 Barely Awards, a three-hour gala at the Duncan Dozenuts Financial Arena. Ralph Munchcut seizes hosting duties to roast Leonard Crum and preside over categories including Best Metaverse (Juicy Verse), Best Trailer (Battletruck/Sanic tie), and Best Guest (Kendelle). Trip Bradstein works the red carpet while a parody Joe Rogan reflects on a year of shaved-ice diets and wire-entangled producers. The night culminates in a contested Best Host tournament won by Case Blackwell, a surprise Lifetime Achievement Award for Pepito featuring a voice memo from Fiona, and a chaotic freestyle finale from Goblin Pisa Hut before a raid on Jim Tasty.
