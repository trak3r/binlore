## Overview

Case Blackwell hosts a shambolic Friday news broadcast with a co-host whose identity shifts via manager texts (Jay→Drilla→Gibbnen→Job Startup) and an intern who floods the studio electronics. The news desk covers State Department mass firings, Asmongold's streaming crown, MrBeast's creator collab, and a suspect Swedish hormone-gel micro-penis story. Chet interrupts for a Science Eyes segment on a sunburn-simulating skin phone case. Trip Bradstein broadcasts from the park bushes interviewing a Hamtaro Antarctic missionary, a phished flower ranger, and a self-proclaimed Zod. The show culminates in an extended Monkey D. Luffy diss-track recording session against Naruto, produced by a ghostwriter who only speaks English, ending with a raid to Christine.
