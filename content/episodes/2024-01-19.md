## Overview

Case Blackwell anchors a 12-hour Lethal Company marathon — 'Fi's 12 Hour Cream Company' — wrangling a 30-player modded lobby through persistent desync crashes, a full mod-list overhaul, and multiple quota runs. Executive Producer Pepito debuts as 'Papito,' a dog-mod speedrunner who survives by sprinting into darkness while freestyling. Crum joins mid-stream, dies spectacularly to bees and Brackens, and challenges Munch to a 1v1 rematch. The crew invents a spray-paint navigation system to escape the facility labyrinths, harvests a record beehive haul, and clears a massive quota before Case breaks for dinner with plans to resume alongside Kendelle.
