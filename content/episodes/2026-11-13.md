## Overview

Barely Informed News devoted the bulk of its telecast to the nationwide FAA ground stop, carrying Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy's evening briefing in full as he blamed overloaded legacy switches, ruled out a cyber attack, and demanded roughly $50 billion in emergency funding from Congress. Hype Train interrupted the briefing as a transportation-themed variant before running out of both fuel and rhymes, and the debate desk later ruled air traffic control overrated in Munch's favor while the channel-point prediction sat unresolved. Chat news closed the news block with a record-setting mycelium kayak, a rescued Florida bobcat, and a honeymoon hippo attack, after which the remote gaming dispatch played The Lion King on keyboard with cheat codes.
