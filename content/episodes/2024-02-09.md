## Overview

With the entire regular correspondent roster mysteriously ill, Case Blackwell drafts his friend Andy and a parade of siblings — Chat's brother Brett, Crum's brother Come — to fill every on-air role. The broadcast careens from a Biden press-conference recap into an absurdist presidential interview, a park-side reporting segment sabotaged by a shape-shifting mic-juice addict, and a debate where Come defeats Munch by arguing gravity makes 'down' morally superior. The night closes with Case dissecting a content-farm movie recap and Chris defending the Knuckles trailer as a certified banger.
