## Overview

Barely Informed News returns to the municipal simulator Renerton for a marathon session spanning seven mayoral administrations across 134 simulated years. Executive producer Pepito opens as a canine strongman before yielding to a parade of network personalities — Kasem G, Crum, Chet, and finally Peter Gibbon — each reshaping the city through bulldozer governance, casino economies, and escalating hospital crises.
