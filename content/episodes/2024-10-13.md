## Overview

Barely Informed News broadcasts a special Renerton mayoral simulation from the They Are Billions city-builder. Pepito opens as acting mayor, achieves 600,000 citizens through aggressive hospital bulldozing, then loses office to Jeb Nogget. Jeb's term ends in a cascade of tornadoes, Godzilla attacks, and nuclear fire. A hot dog sandwich wins the next election unanimously. After the hot dog fails, Peter Gibbon and a shadow cabal force Munch to rebuild from ten survivors. Munch stabilizes the wasteland until Xena Warrior Princess wins a subsequent election, but her term ends when a misclicked soldier triggers total civilian slaughter. The broadcast concludes with a raid to Spinachi.
