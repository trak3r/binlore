## Overview

Case Blackwell returns for a second consecutive broadcast, opening with Rick to dissect Google's $45M Israeli propaganda ad buy and the UN famine declaration in Gaza. Trent and parole officer Chris review trailers for 28 Years Later: The Bone Temple and Marvel Zombies, then Trent defends Speed Racer as cinema's peak in a chaotic In Circle. Peter Gibbon hijacks the news desk with tabloid stories including Putin and Xi discussing immortality on a hot mic. Chet and ChetAI deploy semen taste science after a trigger-word summon. Jeb Nogget forges a J-hook while arguing with his board-trapped father. Munch debates a glitching Crum on propaganda effectiveness. Trip Bradstein interviews a surreal park parade including a twice-appearing Peter Gibbon. Case signs off announcing a Mon/Wed/Fri schedule and a 9/19 D&D one-shot before raiding Gina Darling.
