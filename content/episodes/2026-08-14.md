## Overview

Lead anchor Case Blackwell returns from a year-long absence alongside in-studio contributor Liliana, anchoring a Friday news desk broadcast covering Luigi Mangione's federal guilty plea, covert CIA strikes on Ecuadorian fishermen, Mike Lindell's Minnesota gubernatorial refusal to concede, and various chat news oddities. The broadcast descends into 'Munch & Crum' chaos over whether Hasan Piker needs character work, followed by a guest-hosted 'Amongst the Web' viral clip review. A 'How To with Jeb' segment inexplicably materializes before Blackwell raids a partner stream to end the night.
