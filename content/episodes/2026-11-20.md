## Overview

Barely Informed News anchor Case Blackwell hosted a six-hour Freak Street telethon from his living-room couch, working a single phone line to raise $40,000 for Cigarette Sandwich's 13-short horror anthology 13 Freak Street. The broadcast was hobbled by a crashing laptop, two wrong hotline numbers, and a remote call-center appearance by debate analyst Crum, who fielded no calls across four hours of waiting. By sign-off the network had raised $7,469, the anchor had surrendered his beard to a chat poll, and the desk closed on a handlebar mustache.
