# Grandma Crumble Bottom

![Grandma Crumble Bottom on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/grandma-crumble-bottom.jpg)

**Grandma Crumble Bottom** is an outspoken elderly guest contributor on *Barely Informed News*, best known for her guest-hosting stint on the network's viral video review desk, [[../segments/amongst-the-web|Amongst the Web]].

## Overview

Grandma Crumble Bottom is a fiercely independent senior citizen whose stream-of-consciousness diatribes, unconventional medical theories, and bewildering domestic anecdotes derail any attempt at standard broadcast structure. 

Insisting on the use of her full formal name (*"Thank you for using my full name"*), Grandma Crumble Bottom approaches the internet with equal parts bewilderment and dismissive contempt, occasionally comparing video segments to *"little puppet shows."*

## Personality & Beliefs

### The "Free Sky Laser" (Vision Restoration)
Grandma Crumble Bottom adamantly opposes modern medical interventions such as Lasik surgery, labeling younger generations *"illiterate"* for paying doctors when there is a *"perfectly good laser in the sky."* Formerly legally blind, she claims to have stared directly at the sun for an extended period, which allegedly restored up to 10% of her vision.

### The 48-Year-Old Prankster Son
When lead anchor [[case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]] observed that her hair looked vibrant and less gray, Grandma Crumble Bottom clarified that the credit went to her 48-year-old son. She revealed that her adult son regularly creeps into her bedroom in the middle of the night to dye her hair normal colors as an inexplicable, ongoing prank.

### The Late Husband & "Foot Bunions"
Grandma frequently drifts into reminiscences of her deceased husband. Rather than tender nostalgia, she expresses a desire to have him back purely to settle grievances for the endless years she spent *"cooking him foot bunions."*

### Existential Aging & Automation
- **The Nature of Memory:** Pragmatic about cognitive decline, she views memory gaps as a natural feature of longevity: *"A large chunk of my life is just gone. That's the beauty of aging... Did you ever really exist when you were young?"*
- **Robotic Skepticism:** Unsettled by modern technology, Grandma Crumble Bottom remains deeply suspicious of automation, repeatedly warning that *"robots are taking over everything."*
