**JD Vance** is the Vice President of the United States and a frequent subject of running commentary, relentless audience teasing, and unannounced satirical on-air appearances on *Barely Informed News*.

## Overview

Lead anchor [[case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]] shares an uncanny physical resemblance to JD Vance, sporting a nearly identical facial silhouette, beard, and presentation (differentiated primarily by eye color). Because of this striking likeness, the *Barely Informed News* audience and live chat tease Case without mercy, constantly submitting side-by-side photo comparisons and taunting him whenever he debuts a new haircut or facial hair trim.

Beyond audience ribbing, Vance is treated in the network's universe as an occasional unannounced on-air contributor or remote caller, dropping in to defend controversial decisions, lecture the desk on domestic culture, and share bizarre personal routines.

## Notable Lore & Bits

- **The Striking Resemblance**: Chat relentlessly compares Case to Vance. During [[episodes/2026-07-11|Episode 2026-07-11]], Case debuted a fresh haircut on-air and explicitly expressed hope that the new style would finally put an end to the constant chat comparisons.
- **Combating the Male Loneliness Epidemic**: In [[episodes/2026-07-16|Episode 2026-07-16]] [33:16], Vance made an unannounced remote appearance to defend his Secret Service detail's helicopter requests. He claimed to single-handedly combat the nation's male loneliness epidemic by forcing his agents (Steve Ball, Mike Long-ass, Johnny Astee, Tommy Tall-ass, and Sneak) to stay awake until 4:00 AM playing "original Counter-Strike Knot Source" while drinking Shasta, Fanta, and Samuel Adams concoctions called "Feistas."
