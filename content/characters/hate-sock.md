# Hate Sock

![Hate Sock on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/hate-sock.jpg)

**Hate Sock** (occasionally stylized as **Hatesawk**) is an antagonistic sock puppet guest and recurring contributor on *Barely Informed News*, notorious for insulting newsroom staff and town hall attendees.

## Overview

Hate Sock is a dark charcoal-grey ribbed puppet with large bulging googly eyes and a vivid purple mouth lining. Debuting behind a miniature wooden podium with his own puppeteering backdrop, Hate Sock was brought into the newsroom by lead anchor [[case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]] to participate in the network's community town hall segment.

Bitter, dismissive, and fiercely uncooperative, Hate Sock treats the news broadcast, caller inquiries, and civic participation with sheer contempt.

## Personality & Signature Catchphrases

Despite participating in complex policy discussions and community Q&As, Hate Sock's vocabulary is famously restricted to two contemptuous, biting phrases:

1. ***"You wish."***
2. ***"Dumbass."***

Hate Sock frequently delivers these interjections in rapid-fire succession or combines them into his definitive verbal strike: *"You wish, dumbass."*

Whether Case is explaining network budgetary constraints, fielding a viewer's petition for a community skate park, or asking about personal life, Hate Sock snaps back with one of his two trademark insults. Case eventually attempted to institute a newsroom policy of ignoring the insults (*"I know what you're going to say, Hate Sock, so I'm actually going to stop reacting to it"*), only for Hate Sock to immediately punctuate the silence with a deadpan *"Dumbass."*

## Character Lore & Backstory

### The Town Hall Desk Appearance
During the July 20, 2026 broadcast, Case convened a community town hall with Hate Sock positioned prominently at the desk. Callers and chat members submitted queries regarding local issues—including a proposed Gooters drive-through and funding for public recreation—all of which were summarily dismissed by Hate Sock's two-phrase commentary.

### The Missing Brother
A persistent mystery in Hate Sock's backstory is the fate of his pair. When Case confronted him directly about his family history—asking *"What happened to your brother? Have you ever even looked for him?"*—Hate Sock glared with visible tension before delivering a chilling *"You wish. Dumbass."* Chat participants and viewers have since circulated theories regarding whether his brother was lost in a laundry accident or deliberately abandoned.

### "Hatesawk" Merch Proposals
Due to his hostile reception and instant notoriety among viewers, Case and production proposed official "Hatesawk" network merchandise, exploring whether the station could market branded grey socks with googly eyes to the audience.
