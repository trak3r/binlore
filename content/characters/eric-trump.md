**Eric Trump** is a businessman, political surrogate, and public figure whose ventures and public statements are satirized on *Barely Informed News*.

## Overview

Eric Trump appears in *Barely Informed News* lore primarily as a figurehead for bizarre corporate and technological developments. Rather than conventional commercial ventures, the news desk focuses on his tangential affiliations with speculative defense robotics and corporate advisory boards.

## Notable Lore & Moments

- **Foundation Robotics & Kinetic War**: In [[episodes/2026-07-17|Episode 2026-07-17]], the news desk highlighted Trump's reported role as chief strategy advisor for "Foundation Future Industries" (Foundation Robotics), reporting with satirical dread on the company's humanoid robots being prepared for kinetic military applications.
- **Studio Chat Inquiries**: In [[episodes/2026-07-16|Episode 2026-07-16]], chat brought up Eric Trump during Case's pre-show cold open discussions.
