# Jan

![Jan on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/jan.jpg)

**Jan** (often **Jan the Intern**) is a recurring unpaid intern on *Barely Informed News* — curly-haired podium guest who fields [[../segments/town-hall|Town Hall]] questions, hosts desk fill-ins, and routinely declines Case's offers of full-time work.

## Overview & Persona

Jan debuts in the archive as the subject of DJ C4's hyper-pop anthem *"What Do I Even Do Here"* and returns for rare on-air seats. On [[episodes/2026-10-19|2026-10-19]] she co-hosts Town Hall opposite [[scrum|Scrum]], reveals calf tattoos she hides under pants ("people look silly in shorts"), wishes on a sandal / live-action Winks franchise, needles Case about engagement, and walks out mid-segment rather than accept a hire.

She is distinct from the revolving cast of unnamed or one-off interns (Blark, Jen/Jenna, mass "Jan" incinerator extras in Encircled bits).
