# DJ C4

![DJ C4 on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/dj-c4.jpg)

**DJ C4** is the network's resident song composer, audio engineer, and in-house music producer, operating out of the dedicated **DJC4 Studios** on *Barely Informed News*.

## Overview & Persona

DJ C4 is responsible for composing original tracks, promotional anthems, and official broadcast theme music for fellow *Barely Informed News* correspondents and segments. Broadcasting from the network's wood-paneled DJC4 audio suite—flanked by glowing "Newsic Productions" neon signage and dual circular "DJ C4" headphone emblems—he appears in his signature white-lettered black "DJ C4" ballcap, over-ear studio monitors, and patterned blazer.

Unlike conventional broadcast audio engineers, DJ C4 constructs songs collaboratively and live on air, synthesizing viewer-submitted rhymes via chat commands (`!L to send a lyric`) with cutting-edge AI assistance and generative ghostwriting models.

## Studio Setup & Signature Bits

- **DJC4 Studios**: A professional audio control room equipped with studio monitor speakers, rack-mounted mixing equipment, and glowing amber-yellow neon signage.
- **AI Ghostwriter Collaboration**: Leverages advanced algorithmic songwriting assistants ("the ghostwriter") to rapidly generate chord progressions, rhymes, and song structures on the fly.
- **Audience Lyric Intake (`!L`)**: Direct viewer participation via chat intake commands, weaving spontaneous audience prompts, inside jokes, and breaking news headlines into studio tracks.
- **Correspondent Theme Songs & Anthems**: Regularly drafts and refines theme music and promotional singles for network personalities and commercial merch campaigns.
- **Guest Vocal Sessions**: Recruits network talent into the vocal booth, notoriously collaborating with senior debate analyst [[characters/crum|Crum]] for high-anxiety rap features.

## Notable Lore & Moments

- **The 'Free Stephanie' Merch Drop Anthem** ([[episodes/2026-07-17|2026-07-17]]): Tasked with producing an official network merch-drop track, DJ C4 opened the `!L` lyric queues. When master moderator [[characters/live-in-sleazy|Live 'n Sleazy]] requested "the economy ceasefire violation blues," DJ C4 and the ghostwriter pivoted mid-session into the runaway track "Free Stephanie." He drafted [[characters/crum|Crum]] to lay down rap bars ("get on my mug or a hot butt plug"), prompting Crum's panicked on-air disclaimer to his wife Belolia that he has never met any woman named Stephanie.
