# Guest Correspondent

**Guest Correspondent** is an on-air personality and contributor on *Barely Informed News*.

## Overview

Remote guest correspondent patch-in during Jimothy news segment. Controversial figure who defines his ASMR acronym's S as 'stuff I can't say' and M as 'mutants are bad,' sparking heated exchanges about ableism and racism with Case. Claims raccoons are 'exactly like people' and endorses cutting World Wildlife Federation funding for being 'too woke.' Cut off mid-segment for time constraints.
