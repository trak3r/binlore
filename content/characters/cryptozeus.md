# Cryptozeus

![Cryptozeus on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/cryptozeus.jpg)

**Cryptozeus** is an on-air gaming persona and correspondent on *Barely Informed News*, featured as the host and central figure of the [[../segments/cryptozeus|Cryptozeus]] gaming segment.

Like all characters on the show, Cryptozeus is portrayed by [[case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]].

## Overview

Cryptozeus streams live gameplay of classic retro PC titles (such as Epic MegaGames' *Jill of the Jungle* trilogy) and simulation games (such as *RollerCoaster Tycoon*). He improvises deep, fictional backstory and narrative lore about pixelated sprite characters, provides running commentary, and weaves in fictional sponsor plugs.

## Key Tropes

- **Sponsor Callouts:** Frequent in-game plugs for Gooters chicken wings and "dino fries."
- **Improvised Sprite Lore:** Treats 90s DOS platformer assets and background text as grand cinematic universe canon.
- **Family Lore:** Comedic stories about family members, such as Case's mother speaking in Old English after a bumper-car mishap.
