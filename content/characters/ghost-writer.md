# Ghost Writer

![Ghost Writer on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/ghost-writer.jpg)

**Ghost Writer** (also styled **Ghostwriter** or "the ghostwriter") is the spectral lyricist who co-writes with [[dj-c4|DJ C4]] in the DJC4 studio on *Barely Informed News*.

## Overview

A free-range studio ghost rather than a confined haunt, Ghost Writer claims roughly two centuries of professional ghostwriting and supplies word banks, choruses, and diss-track bars on demand. Broadcast transcripts and wiki stubs variously spelled the name as one word or two; both refer to the same undead collaborator.

On air he is sometimes a cooperative AI-adjacent songwriting partner and sometimes a supernatural heckler who interrupts mixes to critique amateur haunting technique.

## Signature bits

- **Free-range ghost** — Chooses to inhabit the ghost-writer room; not bound like other studio ghosts.
- **Language after death** — Claims to speak only English on the mortal plane (forgot Spanish and other languages upon dying).
- **DJ C4 partnership** — Standard co-credit on R&B, grime, parody, and tribute tracks produced live in the DJC4 block.
- **Heckler mode** — Interrupts sessions (e.g. Trump drawing-denial R&B) with intangible, horny-ghost commentary.
