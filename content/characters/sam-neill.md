## Overview

Best known internationally for his starring role as Dr. Alan Grant in *Jurassic Park*, Neill was honored on the broadcast as the quintessential Hollywood "silver fox." The news desk delivered an affectionate yet irreverent retrospective celebrating his distinguished filmography, rugged cinematic charm, and enduring screen presence.
