**Bryan Johnson** is an ultra-wealthy tech entrepreneur, venture capitalist, and extreme longevity biohacker who is frequently scrutinized and lampooned on *Barely Informed News*.

## Overview

Renowned for his multimillion-dollar anti-aging regimen ("Project Blueprint") and "Don't Die" movement, Johnson's intense biometric monitoring, plasma transfusions, and rigid daily protocols make him a natural target for the news desk's satirical scientific analysis.

The desk covered Johnson's public revelation that despite his exhaustive age-reversal interventions, he had been diagnosed with an incurable autoimmune condition—prompting philosophical and comedic reflections from the newsroom on human mortality and biohacking futility.
