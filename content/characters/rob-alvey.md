# Rob Alvey

![Rob Alvey on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/rob-elvie.jpg)

**Rob Alvey** is a ban-happy theme-park and roller-coaster obsessive who has been showing up on *Barely Informed News* since 2023. He wanders into segments to ban, block, and "cancel" whoever is in front of him, and his favorite target is [[characters/trip-bradstein|Trip]] on [[../segments/trip-on-the-street|Trip on the Street]]. His surname is rendered inconsistently in transcripts (Elvie, Alvy, Alvie), but the canonical spelling is **Alvey**.

## Overview & Persona

Rob is a grumpy, thin-skinned gatekeeper who treats every room he enters as his own park to police. His reflex answer to any slight is to ban the person responsible, often from places he has no authority over (Cedar Point, Orlando, the studio itself). He usually gets banned right back by Case, Trip, or both. Each return is pitched as a fresh start, and each one collapses within minutes.

He looks different every time he appears and takes it personally when anyone says so ("It's insulting that everybody keeps telling me and only me that I look different").

On air, his name has turned into a verb for indiscriminate banning: during the [[episodes/2026-11-10-2925329547|2026-11-10]] news block Case argued "you can't just Rob Alvey everything," then admitted "Rob does."

## Signature Bits

- **The ban hammer**: Bans chat, guests, hosts, and entire theme parks' worth of visitors on sight. When Case and another guest both try to reason with him in [[episodes/2025-10-13|2025-10-13]], he answers, "You are both banned."
- **Harassing Trip on the Street**: Rob is a near-permanent fixture among Trip's park interviewees. He bans Trip and the park crowd, steals the mailed invitation to Trip's son Chase's wedding and blocks Trip's number ([[episodes/2024-06-26|2024-06-26]], where Trip finally microphone-punches him), poses as a fake cop with tape ([[episodes/2025-04-04|2025-04-04]]), bans Case from Orlando ([[episodes/2025-03-07|2025-03-07]]), and keeps trying to negotiate his own unban ([[episodes/2024-07-25|2024-07-25]]). Trip is eventually authorized to "blow his ass away" on sight ([[episodes/2025-06-13|2025-06-13]]).
- **Theme parks & roller coasters**: Everything comes back to coasters. He bans everyone from Cedar Point ([[episodes/2024-05-15|2024-05-15]]), explains the "rollercoaster intestines" theory ([[episodes/2024-06-05|2024-06-05]]), and got re-banned in [[episodes/2025-06-13|2025-06-13]] over a roller-coaster game endorsement dispute.
- **Alvalstonia**: In [[episodes/2023-12-02|2023-12-02]], chat demanded his return and he claimed citizenship of the fictional nation of Alvalstonia, "where roller coasters loop," with an anthem that turned out to be a war dirge for "the day all Alvalstonians got booted out of Six Flags Illinois." He called his banning xenophobia, was banned anyway, and his voice distorted into [[characters/rick|Rick]] on the way out.
- **Failed reform arcs**: He has returned claiming to have turned over a new leaf at least twice ([[episodes/2025-09-10|2025-09-10]], [[episodes/2025-06-13|2025-06-13]]) and reverted to banning both times.

## Relationships

- **[[characters/trip-bradstein|Trip]]**: His main target and nemesis. Rob haunts Trip's bushes, bans the park, and sabotaged his son's wedding.
- **[[characters/robbie-love|Robbie Love]]**: Rival and frequent mix-up. Robbie is the roller-skates guy and Rob is the coasters guy. They argued over whose name was whose on a joint call-in ([[episodes/2025-10-13|2025-10-13]]) and traded verses in a [[characters/dj-c4|DJ C4]] diss track ([[episodes/2025-10-18|2025-10-18]]).
- **[[characters/case-blackwell|Case]]**: Checks in with Rob only under chat pressure and "takes no pleasure in it." Case usually ends the visit by banning him.
- **[[characters/mancini-smooth|Mancini Smooth]]**: Co-hosted a [[../segments/town-hall|Town Hall]] with him in [[episodes/2025-09-26|2025-09-26]] and mocked him throughout.
