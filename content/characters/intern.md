# Intern

![Intern on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/intern.jpg)

**The Intern** is the nameless, unpaid male intern of *Barely Informed News*. He's a young guy with a huge mop of dark curly hair and a permanent five-o'clock shadow, and he gets drafted into whatever job nobody else showed up for. He is pointedly *not* the people's favorite intern; that's [[jan|Jan]]. He maintains, unprompted, that he is "the main intern, though, just so you know."

## Overview & Persona

The Intern is reluctant, literal-minded, and unusually firm about the limits of his job description. He'll fill in for a missing correspondent, but he wants everyone to know he's "just filling in," and that some tasks (like cleaning) are "a little past my responsibilities." He talks back to Case more than an intern should, and nobody, including Case, seems to know his name.

## Lore & Signature Bits

- **Unpaid:** "You don't pay me enough to know this stuff, man." Then, correcting himself: "You don't pay me at all. I'm an intern."
- **Second-favorite intern:** Case reminds him he's "not even the people's favorite intern," meaning Jan. He counters that he is the *main* intern.
- **Nameless:** Case can't remember his name ("What's your name again?" "Dude. We've worked together for how long?"). Chat keeps asking too.
- **Fill-in for everyone:** He has co-anchored the news desk when "nobody's available to do the news" and briefly hosted [[../segments/cryptozeus|CryptoZeu$]] from Brandon's room while Brandon was missing.
- **Brandon's mom's favorite:** She tells him "you're already a better son than Brandon" and asks if he'll replace Brandon permanently. Later in the same segment she claims she "killed that intern" for Brandon.
- **Helium:** Has been caught huffing helium with [[pepito|Pepito]] and [[jeff-ripple|Jeff Ripple]], and denies being a narc.
