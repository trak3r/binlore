**Donald Trump** is the 45th and 47th President of the United States and a central figure in political news coverage, debate fodder, and running comedic commentary on *Barely Informed News*.

## Overview

Trump's political rallies, policy announcements, and legal entanglements serve as constant fuel for the *Barely Informed News* desk, particularly during the fiery [[../segments/munch-and-crum|Munch & Crum]] debate segment. Debate analyst [[munch|Munch]] frequently attempts unhinged defenses of Trump's actions and "integrity" using baroque, nonsensical metaphors (such as "nipple spikes"), which senior analyst [[crum|Crum]] routinely dismantles to the delight of the voting audience.

## Notable Lore & Moments

- **"Mom Donny" & Luxury Communism**: In [[episodes/2026-06-27|Episode 2026-06-27]], the broadcast explored Trump's populist real estate rhetoric, satirically christening his housing and infrastructure propositions as "Mom Donny's Luxury Communism."
- **2020 Election Debate**: In [[episodes/2026-07-16|Episode 2026-07-16]] [57:41], Munch and Crum engaged in a heated debate over the 2020 election results. Munch argued that Trump had won by retaining his "spiritual integrity," leading to complete rhetorical collapse and another audience defeat.
- **Foreign Policy Blocks**: In [[episodes/2026-07-11|Episode 2026-07-11]] and [[episodes/2026-07-13|Episode 2026-07-13]], the news desk scrutinized Trump's foreign policy rhetoric, including proposals for naval blockades and military actions.
