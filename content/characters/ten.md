# Ten

![Ten on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/ten.jpg)

**Ten** is [[characters/trent|Trent]]'s cousin. He arrived unannounced on the [[../segments/at-the-movies|At the Movies]] set in [[episodes/2026-11-10-2925329547|2026-11-10]] while Trent was "showing him what I do." He wears the same black beanie and beard as Trent, and Case noted, "He looks exactly like you."

## Overview & Persona

Everybody calls him Ten, but "his real name is Six." He is sixteen ("Convenient age. It's got a six in it"), and he rates every trailer a six "because I am six."

Ten is extremely sensitive to vibes. Any confusion about his name, any shushing, any slight from chat ruins his entire day. His coping strategy is to leave and kick a tree "until either my foot broke or the tree... First one to break breaks." Ten has never won against a tree.

He insists on respect from everyone, including his cousin: "Don't shush me, young blood. Respect." He has also explained that "shwish" is the respectful way to say "shush."

## Debut

Ten's first visit to [[../segments/at-the-movies|At the Movies]] played out in this order:

1. Nobody could work out whether he was saying "Ten" or "ten," and he tried to leave because "everybody's not being chill."
2. Chat won him back with the nickname **"Battle Arena Toshin Ten"**: "That does rock. I'm back in... I felt like maybe I was at my lowest ever."
3. A chat insult re-ruined his day, so he went out to kick the tree, despite pleas of "Ten, don't let the darkness come for you, man" and "Ten, don't do it, dude. You're gonna break your foot again."
4. He rated *Long Haired Businessmen* a six ("It's a serious rating. Not everything's fucking funny, man").
5. Mid-trailer he announced, "I broke my foot... I'm gonna have to talk to my fucking aunt about this."
6. A few trailers later he broke his other foot as well, kicking the tree from a handstand ("That is impressive upper body strength, but Ten, it's a misuse of it").
7. He left in **Ten's Chariot**, a wheelchair kept chained to the back of an electric bike "just in case he breaks his feet, both of them, kicking trees."

## Relationships

- **[[characters/trent|Trent]]**: Cousin and lookalike. Trent brought him to work to show him the job.
- **[[characters/chris|Chris]]**: Trent's parole officer, who was on set for Ten's debut.
