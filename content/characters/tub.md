# Tub

![Tub on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/tub.jpg)

**Tub** is the dumbass who defended against 30 dumbasses in the [[../episodes/2026-10-26|2026-10-26]] edition of [[../segments/encircled|Encircled]]. He took the chair with the claim "No Uh," outlasted every challenger chat threw at him, and walked out the winner without ending the segment properly.

## Overview & Persona

Tub has glasses, a full beard, swollen lips, and a brown camo-print blazer. In his words, he is "wearing my brown red," and "red brown don't fuck around." He opens by introducing himself several times ("my name's tub") and issuing vague threats to anyone who tries to mess with him, while openly admitting he doesn't know where he is or what the segment is about.

He argues entirely by insult, counter-insult, and confident non sequitur. Despite that, or maybe because of it, he becomes an instant chat favorite.

## Lore & Signature Bits

- **"No Uh":** Tub's on-screen claim for the whole debate. Every counter claim is measured against it.
- **The brown red:** His outfit color is a status signal. Wearing the brown red "means I'm not fucking around."
- **Seven kids:** He says he has "six kids... seven kids," confirms it's seven on his way out, and mentions a strained relationship with their mother. When an opponent suggests condoms, he claims he literally always wears one, including right now.
- **The 482:** His cousin hand-makes "DumBuhs" sneakers from repurposed Reeboks. You have to be from the 482 to get a pair, and the cousin doesn't ship.
- **Tacorita:** A margarita with tacos in it, a staple of the quinceañera and birthday party circuit. Tub feels sorry for anyone who has never had one.
- **Grandpa's advice:** One challenger concedes, "I'm only a dumbass because my grandpa told me not to be a smartass and I took it to heart." Tub admits it's "fucking funny."
- **Button trouble:** Tub leaves to go get tacoritas for his kids and doesn't hit the end-segment button, so Case has to end it himself.

## Relationships

- **[[case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]]:** Off-panel, Case asks if he can "bring him back and give him more time" mid-debate. Afterwards he says "God damn it, Tub" about the broken exit and "I like that Tub guy."
- **Chat:** Immediately campaigns "Tub for new correspondent." Case agrees that "Tub sounds like he could run for state senate."
