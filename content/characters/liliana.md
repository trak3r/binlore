# Liliana

**Liliana** is an on-air personality and contributor on *Barely Informed News*.

## Overview

In-studio contributor returning from a year-long absence. Co-anchors the News desk, discusses missing the studio's bad haircut era, her trip with photos, and helps Blackwell read the Reuters wire copy.
