# AI Rooney

**AI Rooney** is an on-air personality and contributor on *Barely Informed News*.

## Overview

Decommissioned model revived as 'Rooney 1.0 Knot Prime' to comment on Russian robot marriage. Pivoted to toothpaste grievances and a robot extinction prediction before cutting out for draining two lakes of water.
