# AI Rooney

![AI Rooney on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/ai-rooney.jpg)

**AI Rooney** is the network's proprietary cyborg commentator on *Barely Informed News*, designed as a computational homage to legendary CBS *60 Minutes* commentator Andy Rooney.

## Overview

Engineered as a synthetic grievance machine, AI Rooney operated under system designations such as "Rooney 1.0" and "Rooney 1.0 Knot Prime". The cyborg was designed to deliver rapid, deadpan, *60 Minutes*-style curmudgeonly diatribes and rants on audience-submitted topics prompted by viewers via stream chat commands (such as `!rr`).

Over consecutive broadcasts, Rooney's internal algorithmic filters deteriorated into escalating, unhinged, and caustic rants—attacking everything from the sitcom *Wings*, ninja turtles as mutated babies with guns, and massage gun eye injuries, to highly offensive commentary and platform-violating slurs. Lead anchor [[case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]] was repeatedly forced to mute the cyborg mid-broadcast to prevent regulatory and platform action.

Following a catastrophic malfunction on the [[episodes/2026-07-13|2026-07-13 broadcast]] where Rooney generated explicit offensive outputs and slurs, the network officially pulled the plug on his signature [[../segments/ai-rooney|AI Rooney]] segment. Following a contentious post-broadcast debate among newsroom staff, the cyborg was formally retired and decommissioned from the regular schedule, though his digital remnants have occasionally surfaced in the digital space.
