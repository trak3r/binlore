# Baseball Man

**Baseball Man** is an on-air personality and contributor on *Barely Informed News*.

## Overview

Mysterious in-studio guest in a baseball hat who speaks only 'the language of baseball,' repeatedly identifying everyone as baseballs before crashing back to earth from a home run trajectory.
