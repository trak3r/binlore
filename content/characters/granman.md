# Granman

![Granman on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/granman.jpg)

**Granman** is a minor guest host and viral-media critic on *Barely Informed News*, most often summoned to sit in on [[../segments/amongst-the-web|Amongst the Web]].

## Overview & Persona

Granman drifts onto the broadcast as an older, blunt guest correspondent whose specialty is reviewing viewer-submitted clips and issuing abrupt cultural directives. He first resurfaced during the [[../episodes/2026-05-29|2026-05-29]] cold open when Case realized the network had simply forgotten he was still available, then later returned to fully host the web-review desk on [[../episodes/2026-06-18|2026-06-18]].

He is a specialty presence rather than a desk fixture: useful for sock lectures, decimal clip scores, and sudden exits after failed high-fives.

## Key Lore & Quirks

- **Athletic Sock Advocacy:** Granman's early signature bit is haranguing "these kids these days" to start wearing athletic socks, delivered with zero follow-up material and quickly judged a weak cold-open clip by Case.
- **Amongst the Web Guest Host:** On 2026-06-18 he scored an XP infomercial (8.09), a Barbenheimer friendship TikTok (7.3), an AI kung fu cat (8.67), a parking-lot fight (6.9), and a demonic Furby testimony (10/10).
- **Reproductive Claim:** Asserts asexual reproduction by laying eggs in his mouth.
- **Therapist Theory:** Chat briefly speculated he might be Crum's wife's sex therapist; the network neither confirmed nor denied.
- **Exit Protocol:** Known to depart abruptly after a failed high-five with Case.
