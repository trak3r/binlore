# Rick

![Rick on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/rick.jpg)

**Rick** is a veteran field correspondent and tactical media analyst on *Barely Informed News*, serving as anchor of the network's viral media evaluation desk, [[../segments/amongst-the-web|Amongst the Web]].

Characterized by a weathered, stern expression and military-grade discipline, Rick brings uncompromising tactical scrutiny to viral internet oddities and viewer-submitted video files.

## Overview

Rick joined the broadcast on the [[episodes/2026-09-04|2026-09-04 broadcast]] to take command of [[../segments/amongst-the-web|Amongst the Web]]. Drawing upon an extensive operational background, Rick evaluates absurd modern internet culture through the lens of strategic threat assessment and surveillance, reviewing user submissions while monitoring audience sentiment scores.
