# Rick

![Rick on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/rick.jpg)

**Rick** is an on-air correspondent on *Barely Informed News*, portraying a grizzled "former military guy" who serves as the host for the community video review segment [[../segments/amongst-the-web|Amongst the Web]].

Portrayed by [[case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]] using face filters and a stern demeanor, Rick provides deadpan, tactical commentary over viewer-submitted internet clips and chat memes.

## Overview

Rick debuted on the [[episodes/2026-09-04|2026-09-04 broadcast]] to helm [[../segments/amongst-the-web|Amongst the Web]]. He approaches absurd internet culture with severe tactical seriousness, reacting to odd viral videos while managing chat rating scores.
