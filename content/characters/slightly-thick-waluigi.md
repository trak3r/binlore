# Slightly Thick Waluigi

**Slightly Thick Waluigi** is a spontaneous one-off interview subject on *Barely Informed News*.

## Overview

Appearing at the end of the [[episodes/2026-09-01|2026-09-01 broadcast]], "Slightly Thick Waluigi" is a Brooklyn-accented call-in persona claiming to be a thick variant of Waluigi who assassinated both Tupac Shakur and JFK using Mario Kart banana peels.
