# High-Velocity Cultural Correspondent

**High-Velocity Cultural Correspondent** is an on-air personality and contributor on *Barely Informed News*.

## Overview

Hype Train is notably absent and described as 'still taking a nap' or 'napping' throughout the broadcast. Multiple references to Hype Train's non-appearance, with Case speculating it started earlier in the day or has high standards. A caller impersonates 'normal hype train' during Streets segment. Raid is sent to Hype Train at broadcast close.
