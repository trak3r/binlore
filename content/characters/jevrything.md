# Jevrything

![Jevrything on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/jevrything.jpg)

**Jevrything** (currently; ask again tomorrow) is a self-proclaimed teen pop sensation, "generational talent," and the network's most surgically altered musician. He debuted as **Jay Drilla** and has since cycled through more names than anyone on *Barely Informed News* can keep track of — least of all lead anchor [[characters/case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]].

## Overview

Jevrything drops into the studio as a news co-anchor, [[segments/amongst-the-web|Amongst the Web]] co-host, or [[segments/town-hall|Town Hall]] panelist, and immediately treats the broadcast as a promotional opportunity: every headline is a lead-in to a new single, every audience question is answered with an album plug. He wears a purple floral blazer, is constantly compared to Justin Bieber (he finds this rude), and insists he is "really young."

He usually arrives trailed by a swarm of flies — "I still have the horde of flies that follow me about" — which Case refers to as his "Legion of Bugs." Jevrything prefers to think of them as fans.

## The Name

Jevrything's signature bit is that **his name is never the same twice, and Case is always saying it wrong.**

Each appearance opens with the same ritual. Case greets him by whatever he was called last time; Jevrything corrects him with the name he is going by *today*, usually announced via a text from his manager, a consulting firm, or "what everybody's saying." The new name is typically a near-homophone of the old one, spelled in a way that cannot be derived from its pronunciation ("It's actually just Jeznery right now." / "How do you spell that?" / "You don't."). When Case finally says it correctly, the name has already changed.

The gag is compounded by a running confusion with the network's actual Jeff, [[characters/jeff-ripple|Jeff Ripple]]: "It's really close to Jeff." / "Yeah, and a plane is close to a jet." For a while the fans "shortened it to Jeff," and the speech-to-text archive of the show has been mistaking the two ever since.

## Surgeries & Body Modifications

Jevrything's other defining trait is an endless series of cosmetic procedures, almost always pitched as beauty or skin treatments, that leave him unable to do something basic. Chat reports he is nearly done with his "plastic surgery punch card" for a free sandwich, and that he "doesn't have normal bones anymore."

- **Song installation surgery** on his thyroid, which "sort of limits my vocal range," plus a second procedure so that he always sounds auto-tuned (2025-06). He cannot smile and must stretch his face daily so it doesn't seize up.
- **Bug removed from his eye** (2025-07-12).
- **30% snake throat**, installed to improve his vocals; "by the end of the year large amounts of my tissue will be snake, on the inside only." He is "one of the throat goats" (2025-09-13).
- **Face melting off the side** mid-broadcast; "good thing I still look okay from the front" (2025-09-05).
- **Shark/snake gel** treatment and **bone excision** to thin his bones "so that my skin is more lustrous." Wears a hat "to keep my head in" so his scalp doesn't slough off; grows back hair only when gorilla skin is grafted on to exfoliate (2025-11-18).
- **Holographic teeth** made of light ("they don't bite very good"), **sand lips**, **"mellow eye" surgery** (marshmallows painted like eyes, leaving him functionally blind), a **deep-fryer** skin treatment, and removal of his ability to look or sound sad — not a problem "if you do happy songs" (2026-02-06).
- **Paper-mâché jaw**, restricting him to non-solids (he steals [[characters/chet|Chet]]'s shake); possibly a **nose converted to a more "efficient" orifice** (2026-03-27).
- **Road salt bone marrow** (bones now ~30% salt, for clear skin), a 23andMe result of "100% bleach," and **vocal cords removed** to fix his formerly deep voice (2026-04-01).
- **Surgeon "mechanisms"** that periodically assemble around him to keep everything in order (2026-05-12).
- **Piano-wire vocal cords**, which occasionally leave him unable to talk; asks whether he can "girth out" his cords (2026-05-29).
- **Custom whale-bone genitals** — "really tidy down there," going for "more symmetry than size" (2026-06-29).
- **Three years of cryotherapy** in his 20s, for skin that is "nice and plump but hard"; skipped COVID entirely (2026-07-17).
- A **"diaphragm module"** — the hole in him he calls his DMs, now his only way to eat (2026-07-24).

## Music & Discography

Jevrything's catalog is vast, heart-obsessed, and almost entirely unreleased. His release strategy has included flash drives sold in a Best Buy parking lot, a barcode in one bowling alley in Des Moines, prompts on Google Gemini, "Laud" (not Claude), singing in the background of strangers' selfie videos, and hiding CDs in poultry ("You can't put a hyperlink in a chicken, Case"). By mid-2026 "99% of my songs are actually now AI," at a rate of roughly 3,000 songs a day.

Titles plugged on air include:

- **Singles:** "Party Down," "Two Becomes Heart," "Break That Heart Wall Down," "Youth" (his attempt to break into the Chinese pop market), "Small Town Kids," "Every Day Is a Perfect 10" (sold to Walgreens), "Style's Too Hot," "Unique New York," "Wave Scooter" (like surfing, but with a scooter), "Forever Not Adulting," "Hungry Eyeball," "Boring Is Just Another Word for Your Heartbreak," "AJ Nothing but a Big Old Sack of DNA," "I Like to Be Scared of the Things That Break."
- **Albums:** *Two Time Pass* (his third), *Everything Is Everything* (the title of three of his albums), two stealth country albums, the spoken-word *Screaming into the Waterfall*, *Love That Make You Man*, *Steam Shirt No Pants* (cover: his shirt is steam), *45 Months*, *46 Months*, and *You're Part of the Problem*.
- **Features:** the bridge of [[characters/dj-c4|DJ C4]]'s mayonnaise parody "Mayo Slather" with [[characters/mancini-smooth|Mancini Smooth]] (2025-08-02), and a verse on [[characters/hype-train|Hype Train]]'s clown track (2026-05-22).

His style shifts as often as his name: earnest teen pop, a "dark and twisted" phase, soft pop ballads, a 90s hip-hop detour, and repeated threats to "go conservative."

## Relationships

- **[[characters/case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]]:** His long-suffering anchor, who cannot remember, pronounce, or spell any of his names and keeps reminding him that music is [[characters/dj-c4|DJ C4]]'s job — "Our job here is the news." Jevrything maintains that Case "just doesn't understand art" and has "too many emotions."
- **[[characters/marfus-darfus|Marfus Darfus]]:** A fellow vocalist Case cannot tell apart from him ("There's one cent of difference between you"). Jevrything insists they "sound very different, actually," and a Darfus–Jevrything collab has been promised.
- **[[characters/gianna-wettmichel|Gianna Wettmichel]]:** Fellow Town Hall panelist (2025-11-18) who enjoys that he gives back her guff and asks the panel to please stop plugging albums.
- **Everything Bagel** and **Jay Nothing:** His Town Hall co-panelists of 2026-04-01 and 2026-05-22 respectively. Jevrything out-philosophizes the bagel, and considers the void-voiced Jay Nothing a beautiful collaborator.
- **[[characters/pepito|Pepito]]:** Approached about management ("Yo, Pepito, I'm open to changing management").
- **[[characters/dr-chath|Dr. Chath]]:** Counseled him through the flies, his father, and loneliness in his debut [[segments/therapy|Therapy]] session: "Embrace the flies."
