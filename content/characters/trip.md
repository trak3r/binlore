# Trip

**Trip** is an on-air personality and contributor on *Barely Informed News*.

## Overview

Field correspondent broadcasting from a park bush. Just woke from a 'two-month bush hibernation.' Conducts 'Trip on the Streets' interviews with bizarre park citizens (his mother, Hype Train, Lol from Wings, George W. Bush, Banana Fana).
