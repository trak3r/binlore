# Jazz Shrimp

![Jazz Shrimp on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/jazz-shrimp.jpg)

**Jazz Shrimp** is a minor culinary-music correspondent and debate provocateur on *Barely Informed News*, best known for defending the claim that he is the greatest jazz shrimp of all time against a rotating panel of food critics.

## Overview & Persona

Broadcasting from a white plate beside a ramekin of dipping sauce, Jazz Shrimp appears as a golden tempura-fried shrimp with a human face grafted onto the curled body. He debuted during the [[../episodes/2026-05-29|2026-05-29]] [[../segments/amongst-the-web|Amongst the Web]] block in a segment billed as **Jazz Shrimp vs. Food Critics**, where viewers submitted counter-claims via `!cc` while thirty critics attempted to dismantle his greatness.

Despite Case teasing early in the telecast that "Jazz Shrimp will be a regular correspondent soon enough," the shrimp remains a minor specialty act rather than a desk fixture—arriving only when the network needs a seafood-and-saxophones identity crisis.

## Key Lore & Quirks

- **The Central Claim:** Jazz Shrimp's entire broadcast persona rests on a single unfalsifiable thesis: *"I'm the greatest jazz shrimp of all time."* He invites food critics to make counter-claims, then treats every challenge as already defeated.
- **Veins Over Batter:** He rejects battered shrimp (especially Long John Silver's) because the casing obscures the "succulent body" and the veins—which he considers essential to the dining experience.
- **British Aristocracy Beef:** One critic accused him of stealing vaguely British aristocratic musical culture; Jazz Shrimp responded with a wah-wah jazz riff and the deadpan dismissal *"I don't know man I'm just a jazz shrimp."*
- **Bug Allegations:** Another critic insisted shrimp are wet cockroaches and that Jazz Shrimp's music is "bug music." The shrimp replied with a love-song freestyle ("sweetest shrimp in the sea… put me in your mouth and breathe me") before insisting shrimp are more than bugs.
- **Dramatic Exits:** Critics who lose the room are "voted out by the group" and occasionally slide off-camera while claiming they were "bred to fall far distances."
