# Jeff Ripple

**Jeff Ripple** appears on *Barely Informed News*. Like all on-air personas, he is portrayed by [[case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]] using face filters and voice changers.

## Overview

On-air character and news/chat reader who trades banter and jabs with other personas (such as [[hype-train|Hype Train]]). Automated speech recognition (ASR) frequently mistranscribes his name as "Jeff Hooper."
