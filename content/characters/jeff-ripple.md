# Jeff Ripple

![Jeff Ripple on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/jeff-ripple.jpg)

**Jeff Ripple** is a studio newsreader and community correspondent on *Barely Informed News*.

Stationed alongside the primary newsroom desk, Ripple monitors incoming viewer dispatches, delivers offbeat news bulletins, and provides sharp on-air commentary alongside lead anchor [[case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]].

## Overview

Ripple serves as the network's human-interest reporter and live audience conduit. He reads breaking viewer reactions to major headlines, tracks eccentric domestic stories, and frequently trades barbs with both Blackwell and high-velocity cultural correspondent [[hype-train|Hype Train]].

Due to automated closed-captioning idiosyncrasies across network feeds, Ripple's surname is frequently transcribed as "Jeff Hooper" or "Ben Hooper."
