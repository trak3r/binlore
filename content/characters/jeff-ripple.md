# Jeff Ripple

![Jeff Ripple on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/jeff-ripple.jpg)

**Jeff Ripple** is a recurring on-air character and news/chat reader on *Barely Informed News*. Like all on-air personas, he is portrayed by [[case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]] using digital face filters and distinct styling.

## Overview

Jeff Ripple functions as an in-studio correspondent and live news/chat reader. He frequently reads out breaking community chatter and viewer comments, reacting to live headlines and trading sharp banter with [[case-blackwell|Case]] and [[hype-train|Hype Train]]. Automated speech recognition (ASR) frequently mishears and mistranscribes his name as "Jeff Hooper."
