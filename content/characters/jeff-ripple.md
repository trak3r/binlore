# Jeff Ripple

![Jeff Ripple on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/jeff-ripple.jpg)

**Jeff Ripple** is a studio newsreader, human-interest correspondent, and community liaison on *Barely Informed News*.

Stationed alongside the primary newsroom desk, Ripple monitors incoming viewer dispatches, delivers offbeat news bulletins, and provides sharp on-air commentary alongside lead anchor [[case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]].

## Overview

Ripple serves as the network's human-interest reporter and live audience conduit. He reads breaking viewer reactions to major headlines, tracks eccentric domestic stories, and frequently trades barbs with both Blackwell and high-velocity cultural correspondent [[characters/hype-train|Hype Train]].

Despite positioning himself as a serious newsroom correspondent in the mold of a legacy Jon Stewart, Ripple's journalistic ambitions are constantly derailed by chat trolling, technical difficulties, and his bitter professional obsessions.

## The Bitter Rivalry with Ben Hooper

Ripple's defining professional obsession is his bitter, one-sided rivalry with **Ben Hooper**. 

Hooper is a real-life reporter (notably known for UPI's Odd News wire) who covers corny, wholesome, and absurd human-interest topics—such as dog surfing competitions, runaway livestock, and quirky Guinness World Records—which reliably produce viral video hits. Hooper is not a character on *Barely Informed News*, has never appeared on the broadcast, and is almost certainly completely unaware that *Barely Informed News* even exists.

Nevertheless, Ripple harbors a deep, boiling hatred for Hooper. The *Barely Informed News* audience and live chat constantly fawn over Hooper's corny reporting style and celebrate his antics specifically to infuriate Ripple. Whenever Blackwell plays a Ben Hooper UPI audio package on-air or chat floods the prompter with praise for Hooper's stories, Ripple is driven to visible jealousy, alternately fuming at the audience's lowbrow taste or sarcastically proclaiming through gritted teeth that he is "the number one Ben Hooper fan."

## Key Eras & Bits

- **The "Jam EFE" Era**: In [[episodes/2026-06-29|Episode 2026-06-29]], Ripple declared he was entering his "Jam EFE" (or "Jeff Everything") era, demanding Case acknowledge "I'm Jeff Ripple" as his network catchphrase, claiming custom whale-bone physical augmentations, and promoting his upcoming album *Steam Shirt No Pants*.
- **The Monday Correspondent**: Positioned himself as the network's dedicated Monday desk anchor, occasionally missing shifts due to extended vet appointments for his cat.
- **Weekend Odds and Ends**: Anchors feature segments curating bizarre North American regional stories (e.g., British Columbia floating islands, Virginia bat encounters, and Tarzana wildlife brawls).
