# DJ Newsic

![DJ Newsic on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/dj-newsic.jpg)

**DJ Newsic** is the network's resident news-singing DJ, electronic music artist, and rapid-fire broadcast closer, commonly dispatched to conclude Friday and late-night editions of *Barely Informed News*.

## Overview & Persona

Sporting messy, electric bright blue hair ("extremely blue haired out and ready to infuriate conservative commentators") and reflective iridescent sunglasses, DJ Newsic brings a vibrant, hyper-stylized energy to the anchor desk. Dressed in a striking patterned blue blazer, he sits directly before the studio condenser microphone, dialing in pulse-pounding techno beats and cranking up heavy auto-tune.

His signature broadcast mission is "singing the news"—specifically fast-tracking and vocalizing the bizarre, uncomfortable, or hilarious news stories that lead anchor [[characters/case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]] "was too afraid to click on" during the primary news block.

## Studio Setup & Signature Bits

- **Electric Blue Hair & Iridescent Eyewear**: DJ Newsic's distinctive visual identity, designed to deliver high-octane club energy straight to the newsroom.
- **Heavy Auto-Tuned Vocals**: Delivers all news updates, commentary, and conversational banter through drenched pitch-correction and vocal synthesizers, proudly declaring his voice "totally natural and beautiful."
- **Driving Techno Beats**: Fast-tracks political headlines, oddity news, and chat observations over high-tempo electronic instrumental loops.
- **"Stories Case Was Afraid to Click On"**: Operates as a late-broadcast repository for taboo, absurd, or legally precarious stories avoided earlier in the stream.
- **Bizarre Studio Guests**: Frequently attempts to book remote or metaphysical guests to weigh in on his lyrical topics—such as an "ant-eater" who turned out to be an outspoken elephant.

## Notable Lore & Moments

- **The Korean Ant-Eating Michelin Disaster** ([[episodes/2026-07-23|2026-07-23]]): Emerging from beneath the desk to close out the broadcast, DJ Newsic delivered an impassioned auto-tuned techno performance regarding a Michelin-starred restaurant in South Korea facing legal peril for serving ants as a dessert garnish. After passionately proclaiming that "love is the only ingredient that should be legal," he introduced a special guest ant-eater who turned out to be an elephant, sparking an unhinged closing musical track before leading the audience into the evening raid.
