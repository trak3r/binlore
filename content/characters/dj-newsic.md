# DJ Newsic

![DJ Newsic on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/dj-newsic.jpg)

**DJ Newsic** (also known as **DJ C4**) is the network's resident electronic music producer, beat-smith, and rapid-fire news jockey, commonly dispatched to close out Friday broadcasts of *Barely Informed News*.

## Overview & Persona

Broadcasting from the dedicated **DJC4 Studios**, DJ Newsic commands the broadcast console with heavy over-ear studio monitors, a custom "DJ C4" cap, and an abstract patterned blazer. He specializes in spinning driving techno beats while fast-tracking breaking news developments, transforming dull policy updates and newsroom drama into pulse-pounding electronic anthems.

During his signature Friday closing sessions, DJ Newsic actively interfaces with the live chat audience, using an automated lyric-intake system (`!L to send a lyric`) to crowd-source lines, hooks, and themes for real-time merch-drop singles and topical anthems.

## Studio Setup & Signature Bits

- **DJC4 Studios**: DJ Newsic operates from an acoustically treated studio dominated by an illuminated amber-yellow "Newsic Productions" neon centerpiece flanked by twin circular "DJ C4" headphone emblems.
- **Fast-Tracking News over Techno**: Rather than standard anchor desk delivery, DJ Newsic synchs current political developments and viewer talking points directly to high-BPM synth tracks, delivering the news at an exhilarating club tempo.
- **Audience Lyric Crowdsourcing (`!L`)**: Viewers submit rhymes and news hooks directly into the broadcast teleprompter to assemble impromptu studio recordings.
- **Merch Drop Anthems**: Serves as the production mastermind behind network marketing soundtracks, producing promotional bangers for official *Barely Informed News* merchandise.
- **Guest Vocal Collaborations**: Frequently recruits reluctant network analysts—most notably senior debate commentator [[characters/crum|Crum]]—to record rap verses, hypeman ad-libs, and pre-emptive disclaimers over his mixes.

## Notable Lore & Moments

- **The 'Free Stephanie' Merch Drop Anthem** ([[episodes/2026-07-17|2026-07-17]]): During his Friday finale, DJ Newsic gathered chat lyrics to record an official network merch track. When technical moderator [[characters/live-in-sleazy|Live 'n Sleazy]] requested "the economy ceasefire violation blues," DJ Newsic pivoted mid-session into the runaway viral banger "Free Stephanie." He brought in [[characters/crum|Crum]] to lay down verses ("get on my mug or a hot butt plug"), accompanied by Crum's panicked on-air disclaimer to his wife Belolia that he has never met anyone named Stephanie.
