# Jeb's Dad

![Jeb's Dad on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/jebs-dad.jpg)

**Jeb's Dad** (formally referenced as **Mr. Noggin**) is the deceased patriarch of the Noggin household and the spectral co-host of the instructional carpentry segment [[../segments/how-to-with-jeb|How To with Jeb]]. His soul is perpetually bound within a single upright rectangular plank of unfinished construction timber.

## Overview & Persona

Appearing at the workshop bench alongside his son [[jeb|Jeb Noggin]], Jeb's Dad communicates as an animated, faint spectral sketch embedded directly into the wood grain. Despite having no lungs or vocal cords, he maintains an abrasive, cantankerous, and hyper-critical presence, providing biting commentary on workshop safety, joinery techniques, and his son's perpetual life shortcomings.

He displays a profound, open disdain for Jeb's woodworking skills and emotional intelligence, constantly comparing him unfavorably to his golden-child brother, **Jet Noggin**.

## Key Attributes & Lore

- **Spiritual Timber Vessel:** Manifests inside a standard 2x4 (or wider board) resting upright on the workbench. His face appears as a floating, etched illustration within the knots of the pine.
- **The Patricide Implication:** It is heavily implied throughout the workshop broadcasts that Jeb himself murdered his father and ritually bound his restless soul into the timber to force him into co-hosting instructional cable-access television. However, neither Jeb nor the board has ever outright confirmed the criminal specifics on air.
- **Parental Favoritism (Jet vs. Jeb):** Never misses an opportunity to sing the praises of Jeb's brother, Jet Noggin. Whether recounting Jet's superior financial earnings, athletic prowess, or legitimate trade certifications, he treats Jeb as an embarrassing second-choice offspring.
- **Workshop Safety Tyrant:** Frequently heckles Jeb whenever power tools, routers, or table saws are activated, demanding eye protection while actively hoping Jeb cuts a finger off so he can point out improper form.
