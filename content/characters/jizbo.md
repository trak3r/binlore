# Jizbo

![Jizbo on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/jizbo.jpg)

**Jizbo** is a minor returning trial witness on *Barely Informed News*, announced as back from the mountain and notable for answering examination with a conga.

## Overview & Persona

During the [[../episodes/2026-03-07|2026-03-07]] peen.ui IP mock trial, Jizbo is called to the stand after a long absence. He announces *"I'm here. It's me. It's jizbo — back from the mountain,"* claims to still be in great shape, and meets a defense prompt about witnessing himself not doing crime by launching into Gloria Estefan's *"Conga"* instead of testimony.

The court sustains the musical interruption despite Jizbo clarifying he does not believe a question was asked.
