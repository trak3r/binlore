# Studio Newsreader

**Studio Newsreader** is an on-air personality and contributor on *Barely Informed News*.

## Overview

Peter Gibbon takes over Jeff Ripple's news reading duties during the pre-show segment, with Peter doing the news while Case watches. Jeff Ripple is not actively present on this broadcast.
