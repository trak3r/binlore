# Marfus Darfus

![Marfus Darfus on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/marfus-darfus.jpg)

**Marfus Darfus** (frequently introduced as **Marfis Darfus**) is a guest host and recurring contributor on *Barely Informed News*, best known for helming the network's viewer viral clip review desk, [[../segments/amongst-the-web|Amongst the Web]].

## Overview

Marfus Darfus is an eccentric on-air personality whose casual demeanor and unvarnished honesty make him an immediate network favorite. Introduced on the broadcast with his signature greeting, *"Hey, it's me, it's Marfis Darfus!"*, he brings an earnest, slightly bewildered sensibility to reviewing internet culture. 

Lead anchor [[case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]] and in-studio contributor [[liliana|Liliana]] frequently express relief when Marfus takes over the desk, openly stating that having Marfus on air is infinitely preferable to enduring [[munch|Munch]]'s volatile rants.

## Personality & Mannerisms

- **Signature Catchphrase:** Opens segments with an enthusiastic declaration of identity: *"Hey, it's me, it's Marfis Darfus!"*
- **Asexual Philosophy:** Marfus candidly reveals during broadcasts that he does not partake in romantic or sexual endeavors, stating plainly: *"I don't actually do sex... I don't believe in it."* When a pause settles over the desk, he notes that despite the "pregnant pause," he himself *"cannot get pregnant."*
- **Idiosyncratic Decimal Rating Scale:** When evaluating viewer submissions, Marfus employs surprisingly specific decimal scoring rather than round numbers (awarding a `6.44` to a dramatic *Family Matters* scene and a `5.5` to *Love Island*).
- **Philosophical Asides:** Prone to existential commentary while watching surveillance or public footage. Upon witnessing a bewildered commuter riding an airport escalator, Marfus deduced the subject was navigating a profound *"fugu state"* (fugue state) where *"it's really about the journey... he discovered something we'll never understand."*
- **Impatience with Stillness:** Marfus prefers fast-paced, direct action, occasionally urging slow-paced viral submissions to move along, prompting Case to caution: *"Marvis, don't be rude."*
