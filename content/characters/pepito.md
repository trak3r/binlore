# Pepito

![Pepito on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/pepito.jpg)

**Pepito** is the dog that produces *Barely Informed News* (BIN).

Voiced and portrayed by [[case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]], Pepito is represented visually via "Producer Cam" wearing a dark suit with a hairless dog face mask, pointed ears, bulbous dark eyes, and a tuft of white hair.

## Role & Persona

- **Title / Role:** Show Producer (also labeled on air as **Peptron 3000**)
- **Signature Bit:** Introducing the broadcast and opening the stream with variations of *"I'm Pepito, I'm the dog that produces the show, and now it is live, and I'm a dog."*
- **Dynamics:** Acts as the behind-the-scenes coordinator who took over producer duties following the ouster of former producer [[peter-gibbon|Peter Gibbon]].
- **Food & Drink Quests:** Frequently involved in food and beverage bits, such as threatening to drink an entire bag of sangria for the holiday weekend.
