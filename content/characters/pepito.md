# Pepito

**Pepito** is the dog that produces *Barely Informed News* (BIN).

Like all characters on the show, Pepito is voiced and portrayed by [[Case Blackwell]].

## Role & Persona

- **Title / Role:** Show Producer
- **Signature Bit:** Introducing the broadcast or chiming in during production gags with variations of *"I'm Pepito, I'm the dog that produces the show..."*.
- **Dynamics:** Acts as the behind-the-scenes coordinator trying to keep the chaos of the broadcast together.
