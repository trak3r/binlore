**Richard Nixon** was the 37th President of the United States and serves as a recurring historical benchmark for political scandal and deep-state machinations on *Barely Informed News*.

## Overview

In newsroom political debates, Nixon is frequently cited as the gold standard of "competent corruption"—a stark historical contrast used to evaluate modern presidential controversies. The news desk and chat submissions often debate whether Nixon's resignation in the wake of the Watergate scandal would even be possible under contemporary media polarization, noting satirically that Nixon was "simply too old-school to survive the deep state."
