# Scrum

![Scrum on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/scrum.jpg)

**Scrum** (occasionally **Eugene Scrum**; chat-styled **Scrumfuscious**) is the on-air alter ego / temp-hire persona of debate analyst [[crum|Leonard Crum]] on *Barely Informed News* — same bald pate and blazer energy, billed as a separate man when Crum is "out," punished, or moonlighting off the debate desk.

## Overview

Scrum debuts in mid-December 2023 as a one-day temp Munch installs to needle Crum, then sticks around via a year-long contract Case claims to have signed on the spot. He later resurfaces as Crum's news-desk and web-desk alias — reading wire copy under protest, co-hosting Amongst the Web, fulfilling contractual rap marathons, and insisting he is a mononym: "Scrum, period."

Visually he reads as Crum's twin (bald, bearded, blazer) rather than a costume change; the *name* and billing do the split. Chat treats cousin-logic and "Scrumfuscious" middle-name jokes as ongoing lore.

## Signature bits

- **Temp hire / Scrum vs Crum** — Munch champions Scrum as the superior desk partner; chat polls pit Scrum against Crum.
- **Eugene Scrum quit bit** — Early dodge where Crum claims to have quit and reappears under the Eugene Scrum name to avoid debating Munch.
- **"Scrum, period"** — Mononym identity with no middle or last name.
- **Contractual obligations** — Monthly appearances, 30-minute rap sessions, news rundowns read under protest ("words are pointless").
- **News / web desk fill-in** — Wire-reading, Amongst the Web guest seat, crypto-summit mockery while billed as Scrum.
