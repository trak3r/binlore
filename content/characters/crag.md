# Crag

![Crag on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/crag.jpg)

**Crag** (on-air lower-third: **Security Officer Crag**; Whisper ASR usually hears **Craig**) is the ineffectual studio security guard of *Barely Informed News* — hired to keep threats out of the newsroom, and reliably failing at it.

## Overview & Persona

Bald, bearded, and billed as security while looking the part is "honestly half the battle." Crag's signature failure mode is [[peter-gibbon|Peter Gibbon]]: he never successfully keeps Peter out of the building or the walls, and chat regularly debates whether that is dereliction or whether Peter never left. [[case-blackwell|Case]] periodically threatens to replace him; [[pepito|Pepito]] refuses to fire him. Occasional field and desk fill-ins — news co-host, [[../segments/amongst-the-web|Amongst the Web]] guest seat, Trip-on-the-Street security for "JD Vance" — do not improve his clearance rate.

## Signature bits

- **Can't keep Peter out** — Primary job failure; park guest Ann O'Nem once defended him on technical grounds.
- **Looks the part** — Case admits the visual read as security is half the hire.
- **"Lay off the plant"** — Studio lore that Crag's attentiveness suffers accordingly.
- **Rare appearances** — Long absences punctuated by "bring back Crag" chat requests and abrupt desk fill-ins.
- **Moonlighting** — Claimed Ben Affleck security work; briefly attached to JD Vance's detail on a Trip segment ("he can do two jobs").
