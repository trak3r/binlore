# Dr. Chath

![Dr. Chath on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/dr-chath.jpg)

**Dr. Chath** is an eccentric minor-character therapist and mental wellness consultant on *Barely Informed News*, presiding over the network's [[../segments/therapy|Therapy]] desk.

## Overview & Persona

Operating from a dedicated counseling office adorned with framed mental health diagrams ("REFRAMING NEGATIVE THOUGHTS" and "BE KIND TO YOUR MIND"), Dr. Chath fields live viewer prompts submitted via the network's `!h to heal` system. 

Despite his clinical positioning, Dr. Chath is a distinctly minor and highly unorthodox contributor to the broadcast. Rather than conventional psychotherapy, his treatment plans typically consist of baffling professional wrestling analogies, unsolicited dance demonstrations (including clumsy chair-top renditions of the Dougie), and bizarre non-sequiturs that leave his patients—most frequently lead anchor [[case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]]—more disoriented than when the session began.

## Key Lore & Quirks

- **The Oversized Helmet & Pins:** Dr. Chath conducts sessions wearing a large black military-style helmet/hat decorated with an eclectic assortment of enamel pins (including a pride flag heart, an anime face, and a raised fist), which often tilts forward and completely obscures his face on camera.
- **Interactive Chat Prompts (`!h`):** Dr. Chath serves as the conduit for audience mental health queries, reading viewer confessions, anxieties, and bizarre existential questions live on air before issuing his therapeutic catchphrase: *"Healing can soon begin."*
- **The Patient Dynamic:** While Case Blackwell is his primary patient—seeking counseling for broadcast stress, panic over bad haircuts, or editorial neuroses—the therapy desk is open to any network personality or contributor who requires clinical guidance.
- **Premium Billing Rate:** Despite offering confusing advice, misinterpreting basic questions, and frequently wandering off mid-session to get coffee, Dr. Chath bills at a steep $500 per hour.
- **Spectral Apparitions:** Dr. Chath's office seems strangely permeable to the supernatural, with in-studio presence [[kendelle|Kendelle]] known to drift through the background like an apparition to her signature theme music.
