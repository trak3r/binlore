# Peter Gibbon

![Peter Gibbon on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/peter-gibbon.jpg)

**Peter Gibbon** appears on *Barely Informed News*. On-air characters and personas are typically portrayed by [[case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]] using face filters and voice changers.

## Overview

Stout, rat-collecting character with a rocket fixation who wakes up merged with Scum inside Scrotum. Sees through Crum's eyes rifling ham at the grocery store. Hosts 'Amongst the Web' and reads the news for the bulk of the episode. Reveals he 'more or less' was in the military.
