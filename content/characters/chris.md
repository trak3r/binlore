# Chris

![Chris on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/chris.jpg)

**Chris** is the probation / parole officer assigned to supervise truant movie correspondent [[trent|Trent]] during [[../segments/at-the-movies|At the Movies]] appearances on *Barely Informed News*.

## Overview & Persona

Chris is on-set because Trent's community-service broadcast requires adult supervision. He co-hosts trailer blocks, undercuts Trent's hot takes, debates alliteration and animation quality with Case, and occasionally becomes the bit — introducing himself on mic as Trent's parole officer who "has to be here."

Kayfabe treats him as an authority figure stuck in a movie theater; production treats him as Trent's permanent sidekick for film segments.

## Signature bits

- Required probation/parole supervision for [[trent|Trent]]
- Co-host and foil on [[../segments/at-the-movies|At the Movies]] / Trailer Thursday
- Family intel dumps about Trent (steak-shop father, parental discord)
- Prostate / bathroom-schedule jokes that derail trailer talk
