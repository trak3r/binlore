# Durf

![Durf on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/durf.jpg)

**Durf** is an itinerant, laid-back guest host on *Barely Informed News*, best known for taking over [[../segments/amongst-the-web|Amongst the Web]] and the chat-submitted news block. He introduces himself as the brother of former Amongst the Web host Garf Gutter. He drifts in after long, unexplained absences, usually wearing a different hat and carrying some new cosmic accessory.

## Overview & Persona

Durf has long, wavy, sun-bleached hair, a full beard, a wide-brimmed black hat, and a purple-and-black patterned blazer. He speaks in a slow, mellow drawl and greets the audience as "fellow travelers," treating even the most deranged viral clip as a chance for quiet reflection. "The world's on fire, but it feels good to cook."

He considers himself a musician. Case regularly asks him to "just kind of mumble a song" to fill dead air during technical problems, and the chyron-style plugs insist "Durf's music [is] available everywhere."

## Lore & Signature Bits

- **The name:** It's "Durf," "Derf," or "Dwarf" depending on the day. In his words, "it is with an O, but it's pronounced Derf," and "it's Durf or nothing." Case admits he forgot it once, and Durf replies that it's "the only name I do."
- **Garf's brother:** Durf introduces himself as Garf's brother ("This one's DERF," as opposed to GERF). When Case asks where his brother is, or whether his brother is dead, Durf only says "he's around."
- **Long absences, new hats:** He reappears looking "totally different" after not working there "in like a year." His explanation: "Can't keep all your hats in one place" and "people change."
- **Cosmic baggage:** He has returned with "spectral whales" ("the cosmos has been following me around lately") and with a mostly translucent box that Case finds unsettling. Durf points out that Case is "outside it right now."
- **The silent album:** He spent time on an apple farm writing an album that is a funeral dirge "but without songs. Without sounds."
