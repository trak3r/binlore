# Scrotum (Peter Gibbon & Crum Fusion)

![Scrotum on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/scrotum.jpg)

**Scrotum** is the chimeric, body-fused entity resulting from a botched "Freaky Friday" spell cast by sorcerer [[cremus-tremando|Cremus Tremando]] on [[peter-gibbon|Peter Gibbon]] and [[crum|Crum]].

Portrayed by [[case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]], Scrotum speaks with a combined, conflicting consciousness—Peter Gibbon's British military cadence and leg-strength boasts clashing with Crum's desperate raspy panic and grocery store ham-theft memories.

## Overview

During the [[episodes/2026-09-04|2026-09-04 broadcast]], instead of the standard [[../segments/munch-and-crum|Munch & Crum]] debate, the sorcerer [[cremus-tremando|Cremus Tremando]] was summoned from the chat to perform a body-swap ritual. The ritual went awry, fusing Peter Gibbon and Crum into a single physical entity called "Scrotum."

Despite the physiological horror, Scrotum hosted the news desk and took over the [[../segments/amongst-the-web|Amongst the Web]] segment for the remainder of the evening.

## Characteristics & Lore

- **Dueling Minds:** Peter Gibbon can feel Crum's thoughts and sees through his memories (e.g. Crum rifling through sliced deli ham at the grocery store).
- **Physical Traits:** Combines Peter Gibbon's deadlifting leg strength and military swagger with Crum's bald, sunken-eyed facial features.
- **Cryptozeus Connection:** When asked by chat if [[cryptozeus|Cryptozeus]] hails from the "Cryptoverse," Scrotum revealed that Cryptozeus was banished from the Cryptoverse after "getting sucked into a giant vacuum."
