# Hype Train

![Hype Train on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/hyper-train.jpg)

**Hype Train** appears on *Barely Informed News*. On-air characters and personas are typically portrayed by [[case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]] using face filters and voice changers.

## Overview

Recurring hype-train persona. Centers around a 3D model of Thomas the Tank Engine with a human face, accompanied by an upright black panther. Does Piss Fruit Ninja promo, vaudeville beat requests, Panther girlfriend rap, defends his 'crypto universe' origin, and tries to push the 'Piss Fruit Ninja' video-game pitch.
