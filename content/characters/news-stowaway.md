# News Stowaway

**News Stowaway** is an on-air personality and contributor on *Barely Informed News*.

## Overview

Former producer Peter Gibbon maintains his wall residency throughout the broadcast, hosting Streets segment and fielding callers including Trippy B, Dale Huffy, and Avatar the Last Airbender. Confirms he is still in the walls, never left, and references interdimensional wall portals. Spills battery acid on exit.
