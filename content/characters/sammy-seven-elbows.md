# Sammy Seven Elbows

![Sammy Seven Elbows on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/sammy-seven-elbows.jpg)

**Sammy Seven Elbows** (often shortened on air to **Sammy**; briefly billed as **Sammy Six Elbows** on debut) is a recurring correspondent and guest personality on *Barely Informed News*.

## Overview

Sammy debuted as a surprise guest commentator during Biden press-conference coverage, was quickly renamed from Six Elbows to Seven Elbows, and settled into a recurring desk presence—sometimes as a conspiracy correspondent, sometimes as a casual in-studio contributor billed simply as Sammy. Chat and staff treat the short name and the full elbow count as the same person.

In later lore, the sock puppet [[hate-sock|Hate Sock]] outs himself as Sammy Seven Elbows in sock form—former president, now fabric.

## Signature bits

- **Night Man / Biden haunt** — Claims President Biden resembles the "Night Man" who haunted his bedside since childhood.
- **Epstein cloning desk** — Early correspondent run covering Daily Mail cloning allegations, followed by on-air amnesia and aliases ("Ale Chrome Chester" / "Ale Munchester").
- **The Thing Song** — Flash-poll win triggers an AI Cisco "Thong Song" parody about an illegal pickle-cart past and Italian swag.
- **Hate Sock reveal** — Canonical identity claimed by Hate Sock during birthright-citizenship coverage.
