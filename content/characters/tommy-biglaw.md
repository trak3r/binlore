# Tommy Biglaw

**Tommy Biglaw** is a one-off satirical legal counsel persona on *Barely Informed News*, portrayed by [[case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]] using voice modulation and an abrasive demeanor.

## Overview

Debuting on the [[episodes/2026-08-26|2026-08-26 broadcast]], Tommy Biglaw is summoned after chat requests legal advice regarding an unhinged video Case displayed on air. Biglaw bills by the minute ($2,500 per "law" broken) and immediately turns on Case by donning his second hat as his own alter-ego, "Big Tommy Prosecutor."
