# Jake

![Jake on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/jake.jpg)

**Jake** is an occasional studio guest on *Barely Informed News*: a man-child in a purple floral blazer who barely speaks, keeps blasting music the stream can't legally play, and has to be talked out of his worse ideas by [[characters/case-blackwell|Case]].

## Overview

Jake turns up unannounced at the desk and treats it like his bedroom. He talks in short, sulky bursts, sneaks copyrighted songs onto the stream ("Jake, we can't have this much copyrighted music"), dances whenever the beat drops, and offers to help in ways that only hurt himself. Case handles him the way you'd handle a kid brought to work: patient, slightly exasperated, and quick to send him home.

## Signature Bits

- **The cement chair.** On his first visit, Jake glued himself to his seat "so that I'd focus," using what he insists is cement (it's mud on his pants). When Case gives him the rest of the day off, it turns out the cement worked: "I'm stuck." [[characters/peter-gibbon|Peter]] is sent to pry him loose during the break. Afterwards the extraction is summed up as "a mess."
- **Helping the people.** Asked how he'd serve the public, Jake offers to poke his own eyes. He can't keep them open long enough to do it.
- **Unlicensed DJ.** He keeps cueing tracks that are "three different layered copyrighted tracks," and has to be told to wait for the drop before freaking out.
- **Sheltered.** Reviewing a birthday sketch on [[segments/amongst-the-web|Amongst the Web]], Jake turns out to know what sex is but insists people don't masturbate. He also claims to be "Jake from State Farm," which Case denies.
