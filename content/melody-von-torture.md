# Melody Von Torture

**Melody Von Torture** (**MVT**) has been in [[characters/case-blackwell|Case]]'s chat since at least July 2024. They're a viewer, not a character, so this page sits outside [[characters/index|On-Air Talent]].

MVT is one of the broadcast's most hands-on chatters. They built a playable *Mega Man* fan game starring Case, drew a stack of the Bart Simpson tattoo designs, submitted merch, supplied [[characters/ai-rooney|AI Rooney]] with complaints, sent requests to [[characters/cryptozeus|Brandon]], and became one of the first citizens of the [[segments/rebuild-rennerton|Rebuild Rennerton]] Valheim server. On [[episodes/2026-11-07-2922559914|2026-11-07]], chat elected them mayor of [[storylines/rennerton|Rennerton]] over [[characters/crum|Crum]].

## Mega Case

On [[episodes/2026-04-11|2026-04-11]], Case spent most of the broadcast in [[segments/amongst-the-web|Amongst the Web]] playing a *Mega Man* fan game MVT made about him. In it, Mega-Case sets out to rescue [[characters/chet|Chet]] from evil robots, armed with a communicator that is "also a gun" and a "Bin Buster." MVT built it to be easy for Case to set up ("They truly went above and beyond"). Case: "Melody Von Torture, you have truly outdone yourself. I don't know what to say."

The first level took him about an hour. He farmed extra lives off respawning enemies to avoid replaying the opening section ("We're cheating the system, guys"), and called MVT an "evil bastard" when the boss turned out to be the only thing in the game that does real damage. After finally beating it, he said: "Melody Von Torture, it's fair that you made it that hard, because if there were like 10 more of those levels, I would literally be here all night... Some of my best voice acting work ever I did for this game. Melody Von Torture, truly too kind to make an actual game for me, of me, a game of me for me. And at just the right skill level to make me play one level for an hour. You know me too well." He offered to record voice lines or sing over the chiptunes for a sequel, and read out the game's credits ("look at you actually crediting people, something I never do on the stream"). [[characters/kendelle|Kendelle]] and Crum tried it too; Crum quit after one death. Case shouted out the game again on [[episodes/2026-04-14|2026-04-14]].

## Mayor of Rennerton

MVT was the first citizen to join the Rennerton Valheim server on [[episodes/2026-10-23|2026-10-23]] ("Welcome to the town, MVT"). They soon found the press corps ("Oh my god, it's MVT! You've made it!"), and their gathered resources paid for skeleton [[characters/munch|Munch]]'s mayoral mansion: "Fortunately, the bounties of Melody Von Torture and Exterminator have provided for us."

They came back on [[episodes/2026-11-07|2026-11-07]] with Stanton "to save the day," and Munch ranked the pair top of the town's citizens. Once Crum took over as mayor, he fell into a hole MVT had dug: "Melody Von Torture, how could you do this to me?" The next day, MVT out-worked him again ("That was all MVT?"), and he admitted, "I'm not mad at you for digging holes. I've just been falling into them." At the end of that stream, MVT stood for mayor against Crum and [[characters/peter-gibbon|Peter]]. Crum led the early count with 97 votes, but "as the more Melody Von Torture centered counties" came in, Big News called the race for MVT. Crum conceded through gritted teeth: "I am so excited for my new mayor Melody Von Torture... I'm excited to work as a key advisor to Melody Von Torture to see his agenda through."
