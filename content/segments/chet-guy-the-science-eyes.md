# Chet Guy the Science Eyes

![Chet Guy the Science Eyes](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/chet-guy-the-science-eyes.jpg)

The show's satirical science and medical research segment (parodying Bill Nye the Science Guy).

## Format

Case puts on a distinctive patterned purple blazer and brings a physical optical microscope onto the news desk. He analyzes bizarre medical research papers, scientific journal findings, and anatomical surveys with straight-faced pseudo-scientific commentary.

## Key Tropes

- **The Microscope Prop:** An optical laboratory microscope featured prominently in the foreground.
- **Academic Citation:** Reads real or fabricated study titles from medical journals (e.g. plastic surgery breast proportion studies).
- **Tone:** Authoritative academic jargon mixed with absurdly unscientific conclusions.
