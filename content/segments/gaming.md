# Gaming

Live gameplay on the main broadcast by Case Blackwell, in-studio talent and guests, from co-op sessions to game browsing. Brandon's remote retro playthroughs are a separate segment, [[cryptozeus|CryptoZeu$]].

## Format

[[../characters/case-blackwell|Case]] takes the broadcast into a game, often with a co-op lobby of studio regulars and guest streamers. Sessions have included *Lethal Company* (including the Great Lethal Off), *Elden Ring*, *Peak* and *Shift at Midnight*. [[../characters/trent|Trent]] has also played solo runs. Chat suggests titles, and Case browses the store live when picking the next game.

## Key Elements

- **Co-op lobbies:** Case with [[../characters/kendelle|Kendelle]], [[../characters/crum|Crum]], [[../characters/pepito|Pepito]] and guest streamers.
- **Setup on air:** mod configuration, Discord calls and lobby troubleshooting are part of the session.
- **Game browsing:** reviewing store pages and chat recommendations before committing to a game.

## What isn't Gaming

- Brandon's bedroom-studio playthroughs are always [[cryptozeus|CryptoZeu$]].
- The Valheim rebuild of Rennerton is [[rebuild-rennerton|Rebuild Rennerton]].
- Small talk before or after a session is [[banter|Banter]].
