# Town Hall

![Town Hall on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/town-hall.jpg)

**Town Hall** is the recurring audience Q&A desk on *Barely Informed News*. Viewers submit questions with the chat command `!th`, and Case seats one or more guests — correspondents, interns, puppets, sentient foodstuffs, or whoever is still in the building — to field them live.

## Format & Overview

Town Hall is a community call-in block: Case moderates, guests answer, and the queue often breaks mid-segment when commands disconnect, guests walk out, or a puppet says something that forces an early cut.

Typical seating pairs a straight guest with a chaotic one (intern + alter ego, correspondent + puppet, musician + bagel). Recurring bits include skate-park pork requests, network diversity needling, and guests declining full-time employment on air.
