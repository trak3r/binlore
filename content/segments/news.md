# News

![News Segment](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/news.jpg)

The anchor segment of *Barely Informed News*.

## Format

[[../characters/case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]] sits at the news desk in front of a Los Angeles cityscape backdrop, with a rolling **"BREAKING"** ticker along the bottom of the screen and a corner BIN badge. Case delivers a mix of real-world headlines, eccentric local stories, and audience submissions with deadpan comedic satire.

## Key Elements

- **Anchor:** [[../characters/case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]]
- **Visuals:** Studio desk with illuminated BIN logo, digital globe graphics, breaking ticker.
- **Tone:** Deadpan satirical broadcast news.
