# News

![News Segment](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/news.jpg)

The flagship news desk and central broadcast block of *Barely Informed News*.

## Format

Lead anchor [[../characters/case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]] presides over the anchor desk in front of a panoramic Los Angeles skyline backdrop. Featuring an active illuminated desk badge and a continuous scrolling **"BREAKING"** news ticker along the bottom of the screen, the broadcast covers major national headlines, political developments, bizarre domestic incidents, and live viewer dispatches.

## Key Elements

- **Lead Anchor:** [[../characters/case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]]
- **Visual Presentation:** Studio news desk, digital globe graphics, and continuous breaking news ticker.
- **Tone:** Deadpan satirical broadcast journalism and investigative reporting.
