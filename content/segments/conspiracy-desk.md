# Conspiracy Desk

![Conspiracy Desk on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/conspiracy-desk.jpg)

The recurring conspiracy deep-dive desk on *Barely Informed News*, variously billed as **Conspiracy Time**, **Truth Zone**, or a handoff to correspondent [[../characters/guy-redacted|Guy Redacted]].

## Format

Case (or a designated conspiracy correspondent) opens a YouTube rabbit hole — blueberries that "aren't blue," Saturnalia Christmas psyops, secret turtles, Bigfoot thank-you notes — and commits to the bit until the evidence collapses into parody. Later seasons formalize the desk around [[../characters/guy-redacted|Guy Redacted]], who operates from an "undisclosed location" after periods of on-air censorship.

## Key Elements

- **Conspiracy Time / Truth Zone:** Named cold-open for the desk before and alongside Guy Redacted's tenure.
- **Guy Redacted:** Sunglasses-forward conspiracy correspondent; Illuminati-hat era returns; investigates Avril Lavigne replacements, Giza underground cities, and blueberry color scandals.
- **Prior host — [[../characters/clementine-verne|Clementine Verne]]:** Original Conspiracy Time / Unvernished Truth correspondent (curly-wigged man) before Guy Redacted's desk branding.
- **Chat as research assistants:** Viewers dump links; the desk treats ten-view uploads as primary sources.
