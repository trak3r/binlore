# Encircled

![Encircled on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/encircled.jpg)

**Encircled** is the last-one-standing debate gauntlet on *Barely Informed News*: one defender stakes out a claim and must hold it against a seemingly endless line of random challengers. Chat votes each challenger out by spamming **`!rf`** (red flag). When enough red flags land, the challenger drops into a deep pit and the next one sits down.

## Format

- **The intro sting:** Every edition opens with the same montage of "past defenders" stating their odd stances: "I just think soda's better without ice. I just think you should be dead. Music is bad and it's bad for you. What? A sock on the door means a crime is happening. Okay, but I don't really get where I am." Oddly, the voices in the montage are well-known primary characters, while the real defenders are almost always off-the-cuff random characters. Once the sting ends, that random defender appears, usually confused about where they are.
- **The premise:** Each Encircled has its own matchup title on screen (e.g. *Case vs 30 Unpaid Interns*, *1 dumbass vs 30 dumbasses*), almost always one versus 30. The topic, the defender, and the opposition change every time, and many matchups come from chat segment requests.
- **The claim:** The defender's position is pinned on screen as `CLAIM:`. Early editions (Munch, Crum, Kevin) used three claims; later ones settle on one. Viewers submit challenges with **`!cc`** (counter claim), and Case confirms each one in chat ("Counter claim encircled, brace for random selection"). The selected counter shows on screen as `COUNTER:`, and a fresh random character arrives to argue it.
- **The vote:** A **RED FLAGS** banner ("!RF votes challenger out") marks voting. Once the flags pile up, an announcer declares the challenger "voted out by the group" and they fall down the hole, usually still arguing on the way down.
- **The clock:** A countdown timer sits between the two chairs. When it runs out, the announcer calls "Okay, pause, that's time. Please wait in your seat." If the defender is still seated, they survive. The timer has a history of glitching.
- **Hosts who don't know the name:** Defenders routinely fumble the title. Crum welcomes everyone "back to… Circled. Being told that's what it's called," and Chet notes "nobody ever introduces this segment."
- **Exit methods:** The pit is the current way out. Earlier editions used a fire trap ([[../episodes/2025-09-13|2025-09-13]]) and an incinerator chute ([[../episodes/2026-04-14|2026-04-14]]).
