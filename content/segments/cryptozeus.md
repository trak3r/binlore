# Cryptozeus

![Cryptozeus Gameplay](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/cryptozeus.jpg)

The live gaming and playthrough segment of *Barely Informed News*.

## Format

Case shifts away from news commentary to play games—ranging from vintage DOS classics (like Epic MegaGames' *Jill of the Jungle*) to simulation games (such as *RollerCoaster Tycoon*).

## Key Tropes

- **Narrative Improvisation:** Case improvises storylines about background sprites, NPC text, and game lore.
- **Sponsor Gags:** Constant in-game plugs for fictional or off-the-wall sponsors (such as Gooters chicken wings and "dino fries").
- **Family Lore:** Stories about relatives (e.g. Case's mother speaking in Old English after a bumper-car accident).
