# Banter

Unscripted desk chatter between Case Blackwell, in-studio talent and chat: pre-show warm-ups, technical setup, housekeeping and schedule talk, and loose conversation between segments. Not news coverage and not gameplay.

## Format

[[../characters/case-blackwell|Case]] talks directly with chat and whoever is in the studio, usually [[../characters/kendelle|Kendelle]], [[../characters/pepito|Pepito]] or [[../characters/crum|Crum]]. Banter fills the gaps the rundown doesn't: troubleshooting the stream, thanking subscribers, planning the night's segments, plugging the schedule, and wandering off-topic.

## Key Elements

- **Pre-show and technical setup:** audio checks, scene fixes, mods and lobby setup before the first real segment.
- **Housekeeping:** stream schedule, Kickstarter and Patreon plugs, sub goals, segment planning.
- **Desk chat:** personal stories, chat polls and tangents between segments.

## What isn't Banter

- Headline coverage, chat news and wire roundups belong to [[news|News]], as do the cold open and the sign-off.
- Gameplay belongs to [[gaming|Gaming]], or to [[cryptozeus|CryptoZeu$]] when Brandon is playing.
