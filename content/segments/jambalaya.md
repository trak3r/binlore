# Jambalaya

![Jambalaya on Barely Informed News](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/jambalaya.jpg)

A multi-guest "secret blank" debate panel on *Barely Informed News*, debuted during the [[../episodes/2024-07-20|2024-07-20]] eight-hour debate marathon.

## Format

Jambalaya seats several network contributors and/or studio guests against an "infinite inky void" backdrop. One panelist holds a hidden trait or confession (the **secret blank**)—for example a secret Obama voter, secret cannibal, or secret bedwetter—while the others and chat probe with questions until the identity is exposed.

Rounds mix political trivia, culinary absurdism, and interpersonal gotchas. The format was explicitly tested as reusable machinery for future guest-heavy broadcasts, and later inspired Jambalaya-style couch debates when guests commandeer the stream deck.

## Key Elements

- **Secret blank:** One contestant conceals a designated attribute; discovery is the win condition.
- **Panel mix:** Recurring talent ([[../characters/chet|Chet]], [[../characters/liliana|Liliana]], [[../characters/grandma-crumble-bottom|Grandma Crumble Bottom]]) alongside one-off guests (Tyler, Bill, Tim).
- **Chat interrogation:** Audience questions drive the reveal as much as on-panel sparring.
- **Culinary / etiquette digressions:** Side debates (artichoke-as-human, potato-as-human, condiment philosophy, smut vs no smut) often outlast the secret itself.
