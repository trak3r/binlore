# Matt Liddell

**Matt Liddell** (`matt_liddell` on Twitch) has been in [[characters/case-blackwell|Case]]'s chat since at least November 2023, and his name comes up in more than 100 broadcasts. He's a viewer, not a character, so this page is filed under [[fans/index|Fanatical Fans]] rather than [[characters/index|On-Air Talent]].

He is one of the channel's longest-running subscribers: Case counted 28 months on [[episodes/2025-03-31|2025-03-31]] and 33 on [[episodes/2025-09-13|2025-09-13]] ("Matt Liddell, one of the OGs"), and he had a 70-stream watch streak by [[episodes/2026-11-07-2922559914|2026-11-07]]. On air he is best known as the one chatter reliably in [[characters/crum|Crum]]'s corner, which got him blamed for Crum's election wins in [[storylines/rennerton|Rennerton]]. He is also the subject of a long-running bit that started with a "Matt Liddell tie" and ended with Case and the correspondents wearing temporary tattoos of his face. Along the way the cast has made him Case's tax attorney, Crum's "accountant attorney," [[characters/cryptozeus|Brandon]]'s unwanted stepdad, and the namesake of Brandon's *Final Fantasy XIV* catboy. On [[episodes/2026-02-28|2026-02-28]] he was greeted as "Matt Liddell, the man of a thousand muscle faces. I think that's Matt Liddell's nickname. It is to me."

## The Matt Liddell Tattoo

It started as a tie. During the fish-tie debate on [[episodes/2025-05-21|2025-05-21]], chat floated "a Matt Liddell tie," and Case wondered what that would even mean: "Would it be like a little Matt Liddell, a full-body Matt Liddell just as a tie?" Matt endorsed the idea later that stream. Nine months later he turned up on [[segments/trip-on-the-street|Trip on the Street]] ([[episodes/2026-02-12|2026-02-12]]) to ask after it: "There was supposed to be a tie made in my visage... When do I get my royalties?" [[characters/trip-bradstein|Trip]] promised to raise it with Case: "Honestly, I hope he pays you. Fuck that guy."

Five days later ([[episodes/2026-02-17|2026-02-17]]), chat's suggestions for Case's first tattoo landed on Matt. "I'm getting a Matt Liddell tie and a Matt Liddell tattoo. Why? Why do I need so much Matt Liddell on my body?" Case agreed to pay for a cutout tie if someone could design one, but drew the line elsewhere: "I am not getting a nude Matt Liddell apron. It feels like it's in bad taste. To Matt." On [[episodes/2026-03-12|2026-03-12]] the tattoo became a sub goal. Case insisted "I'm not getting the permanent Matt Liddell tattoo" and settled on custom temporary ones, while [[characters/hype-train|Hype Train]] rapped about forcing "a Matt Liddell on your face." Matt sent in photos ("These are the Matt options, everyone") and Case asked [[characters/kendelle|Kendelle]] to wear one with him ([[episodes/2026-03-19|2026-03-19]]). On [[episodes/2026-04-06|2026-04-06]], Crum demanded, "Where's the fucking Matt Liddell tattoo?... I feel like I'm the only one who remembers this shit, Case."

The tattoos arrived in a pack of ten, and Case applied the first one on air in a sleeveless undershirt on "Casual Friday," [[episodes/2026-05-01|2026-05-01]]: "It's fucking huge... Maybe I was being too optimistic about the size of my arms." [[characters/jeff-ripple|Jeff]], [[characters/chet|Chet]] and Kendelle got matching ones. Chet insisted "my Matt tattoo looks even better." The next day "we had the guns out today to show off the Matt tat," and Case joked that a permanent version is "priority number one" ([[episodes/2026-05-02|2026-05-02]]). When the much cheaper Bart Simpson temporary tattoos turned out to be hard to get off, he looked back fondly: "Obviously I went with only the most premium Matt Liddell tattoo" ([[episodes/2026-08-26|2026-08-26]]).

## Crum's Constituency

While the rest of chat was cheering for the Rock-afire gorilla in the [[storylines/crum-face-punch|Crum Face Punch]] bet, [[characters/munch|Munch]] grumbled on [[episodes/2025-06-11|2025-06-11]] that Crum had "one defender... I respect Matt Liddell and his commitment to pacifism, but everybody else wants to see you fucking die." Crum: "It's more than just Matt. There's a whole Crum pack." After the punch, Matt left Munch a threat on Trip on the Street, and in the debut of [[segments/encircled|Encircled]] he challenged Munch over Crum's death. Munch called him "the most formidable adversary." On [[episodes/2026-03-10|2026-03-10]] Crum thanked him "for taking back this station for the people who founded it, me and you, Matt." By [[episodes/2026-09-02|2026-09-02]], Matt was posting in chat as "Crum's accountant attorney," and when Crum threatened to sue chat on [[episodes/2026-11-06|2026-11-06]] he made an exception: "Not all of you, obviously not Matt Liddell."

That loyalty carried into the [[segments/rebuild-rennerton|Rebuild Rennerton]] elections, which run on unlimited channel-points polls ("It is pay to play. One vote, one dollar"). When Crum beat skeleton Munch for mayor on [[episodes/2026-11-07|2026-11-07]], Munch knew who to blame: "You guys keep fucking doing this to yourselves, and by you guys I do mean you, Matt. I mean specifically Matt Liddell." At Mayor Crum's first press conference the next day, Matt checked in before going to pick up dinner ("You're doing an awesome job"). Crum: "Always happy to have your support. What a gosh darn hero." When Crum took an early lead in that evening's election, Case said, "I don't even know if Matt Liddell's around, so I can't tell if that's the usual pay to play that Crum engages in to win these elections." [[fans/melody-von-torture|Melody Von Torture]] won anyway, and Case told the new mayor: "No matter what the Matt Liddell constituency says, you've always been my mayor." Crum's parting words in his concession: "Matt, I know you got the points. Come on. Let's not let it slip."
