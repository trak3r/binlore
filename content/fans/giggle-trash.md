# Giggle Trash

**Giggle Trash** (sometimes just "Giggles") has been in [[characters/case-blackwell|Case]]'s chat since at least November 2023, when Case was already thanking them for "dropping some customary bits." They're a viewer, not a character, so this page is filed under [[fans/index|Fanatical Fans]] rather than [[characters/index|On-Air Talent]].

Their name comes up in more than 100 broadcasts, which makes them one of the most-thanked people in the chat. They're a long-running subscriber (14 months on [[episodes/2024-02-09|2024-02-09]], 22 months on [[episodes/2024-10-22-4xAoUbdxBjE|2024-10-22]]) and a steady source of bits, five- and ten-sub community gifts, gifted subs to other chatters and, from February 2026, donations through the stream's donate button. Their bit messages are regularly read aloud by "the president," [[characters/hype-train|Hype Train]] has sung about them more than once, and from late 2025 they became one of the main suppliers of complaints to [[segments/ai-rooney|AI Rooney]]. They also pitch topics: debate questions for [[segments/munch-and-crum|Munch & Crum]], a "how to deal with having 14 kids" expert, and an animated series idea.

## The President Reads Their Bits

Big bit drops and donations trigger an alert voiced as "the president," in a Joe Biden style ("Listen up, Jack. Giggle Trash just mama jam 300 shiny tasty coins, and I've been dying to say this…"; donations get "philanthropized" in "money bucks"). Then the alert reads out whatever Giggle Trash wrote. The first one on record is on [[episodes/2024-05-21|2024-05-21]]. [[characters/peter-gibbon|Peter]] explains to Giggle Trash how the graphic command works ("How do you know that?" "I watch you from the walls"), and their 300 bits arrive with "Confetti A for Peter." Later messages had the president:

- ask [[characters/jeff-ripple|Jeff]] out for "ice cream and burgers" so they could talk "about hammers and hummus" (Jeff: "That's a hard yes for me") on [[episodes/2024-08-30|2024-08-30]];
- accuse [[characters/crum|Crum]] of having "the best hand game in all St. Thomas, allegedly" on [[episodes/2026-02-17|2026-02-17]];
- declare "today officially Hype Train Day" on [[episodes/2026-03-12|2026-03-12]];
- approve "of this stream applying for partner. Jack, it's the only way to heal this country" on [[episodes/2026-03-20|2026-03-20]];
- give "a personal shout out to Melody Von Torture" during Case's *Mega Man* fan-game playthrough on [[episodes/2026-04-11|2026-04-11]] (800 bits);
- give "one dollar to everyone that didn't storm the Capitol on January 6th" on [[episodes/2026-05-18|2026-05-18]];
- ask [[characters/abraham-lincoln|Abraham Lincoln]], in studio, to "tell the Grim Reaper to get off his fat ass and get back to work" on [[episodes/2026-07-20|2026-07-20]];
- invite Case and Peter out for sushi on [[episodes/2026-09-29|2026-09-29]], leading to an argument over which of them was invited;
- introduce himself as Commander Shepard ("this is my favorite gooners in the Citadel") while skeleton [[characters/munch|Munch]] was building Gooters in Rennerton on [[episodes/2026-11-07|2026-11-07]].

Giggle Trash was also one of the first donors when the donate button went live in February 2026. Half of what comes in through it goes to the Donut Mutual Aid for families in Gaza. After their $20 on 2026-02-17, Case said, "So far we've raised 20 dollars." Their donations pushed the total past $100 on [[episodes/2026-04-09|2026-04-09]] ("It's donation time, baby"), and [[characters/cryptozeus|Brandon]] has thanked them mid-game for donating "to that thing that, like, I guess Case decided is like good or something."

## "Giggle Trash Got Me Thinking"

Giggle Trash supplied the complaint behind AI Rooney's catchphrase "Giggle Trash got me thinking" on at least 12 broadcasts between [[episodes/2025-10-02|2025-10-02]] and [[episodes/2026-07-13|2026-07-13]]. The complaints are usually "back in my day" laments: burgers that "had a thick slab of meat goo inside," the need for "brave pioneers again, like the guy who first drank milk from a cow or the guy who invented Nutter Butters," missing the days "when you could wear leotards on TV," pirates who "used to sing and dance," people who used to chew their cigarettes, and wars "fought over women and stone wheels, not oil and illegal bootleg oven cleaner." Rooney almost always agrees ("You're absolutely right, Giggle Trash") before drifting somewhere else: [[characters/cryptozeus|Brandon]]'s nipples, Crash Bandicoot, or Michael J. Fox's backside in *Spin City*. On [[episodes/2026-04-22|2026-04-22]] he got as far as "Giggle Trash got me thinking" before "Wait, I forgot what I was complaining about."
