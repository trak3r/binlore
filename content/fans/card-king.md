# Card King

**Card King** (`cardking_01` on Twitch, which [[characters/ai-rooney|AI Rooney]] reads out as "Card King underscore zero one") has been in [[characters/case-blackwell|Case]]'s chat since at least December 2023 and is named in more than 160 broadcasts. They're a viewer, not a character, so this page is filed under [[fans/index|Fanatical Fans]] rather than [[characters/index|On-Air Talent]].

Card King is one of the channel's steadiest supporters. They gift community subs in batches, cheer hundreds of bits at a time, and get greeted in the opening roll call. Their cheers come with messages, and since mid-2024 those messages have been read aloud in the president's voice. They have also sent [[characters/clementine-verne|Clementine]] a conspiracy to investigate, requested [[segments/munch-and-crum|Munch & Crum]] debates and a [[segments/dj-c4|DJ C4]] song, written rhymes that broke [[characters/scrum|Scrum]], fed four complaints to AI Rooney, and shared a steady supply of clips for [[segments/amongst-the-web|Amongst the Web]]. Case also reads a lot of their chat out on air, including the one comment that made [[characters/kendelle|Kendelle]] walk off.

## Listen Up, Jack

On [[episodes/2024-05-15|2024-05-15]], Case switched on a new alert: any cheer over 300 bits gets its message read out by text-to-speech in a Joe Biden voice. The template is "Listen up, Jack. Card King just mama-jammed 300 shiny tasty coins, and I've been dying to say this," followed by whatever the viewer typed. The transcripts often garble it into "Card King unjust mama jam." "Just Mama Jam" is part of the alert, not another chatter; the same wording comes before every cheerer's name (Microphone 900, Skymer, Giggle Trash, Big J Dub and others).

Card King's was the second alert ever. Biden recited "Jenny from the Block" ("They know where I came from, from the Bronx"), and Case decided, "I think I like this text-to-speech thing actually, it's providing the chaos." Since then Card King has used the president for dozens of interruptions. Biden has rapped for [[characters/jeb|Jeb]]'s rack-chugging initiation ("the president literally demanded it"), urged [[characters/hype-train|Hype Train]] to "torch the field" and "fuck Jeff Ripple" (Jeff: "That train has it out for me"), slipped acid into [[characters/pepito|Pepito]]'s birthday cheese, outed [[characters/hate-sock|Hate Sock]], announced "I've hijacked the presidency," asked for "triple dancing Kendelles," called Kendelle to the stand at [[characters/crum|Crum]]'s mock trial, and recited a prayer on a How To with Jeb. Case usually answers "What's that, Mr. President?", and once thanked "Mr. President slash Card King" for the bits.

A 1,000-bit cheer triggers a bigger version: "Card King underscore zero one just absolutely pounded 1,000 sweet bits straight into the great chasm of progress, and I just have to say this." On [[episodes/2025-12-11|2025-12-11]] it brought out Obama ("Yo, Joe, sit the hell down and let a real president hold it down"), and Case thanked Card King "for summoning the very secret president."

## Requests

Card King's suggestions turn into segments fairly often. On [[episodes/2024-03-14|2024-03-14]], Clementine investigated the House of Illuminati, the firm behind the Glasgow Willy Wonka experience, at Card King's request. Card King dropped the Wayback Machine link and Clementine thanked them for it: "You said it was silly. I'll say it was anything but silly. I'm deeply disturbed." A week later, 1,000 bits bought the Munch & Crum debate "When AI achieves consciousness, will they leave ghosts when they die?" ([[episodes/2024-03-21|2024-03-21]]). Card King requested DJ C4's Celtic dubstep track "Rockin' in the Green Screen" on [[episodes/2025-05-23|2025-05-23]], a Royal Rumble watch-along, and the "How to Crip Walk for Beginners" lesson on [[episodes/2026-11-05|2026-11-05]].
