# Skymer

**Skymer** (`skymyr` on Twitch) has been in [[characters/case-blackwell|Case]]'s chat since at least November 2023 and is named on air in more than 200 broadcasts. They're a viewer, not a character, so this page is filed under [[fans/index|Fanatical Fans]] rather than [[characters/index|On-Air Talent]].

Skymer is a long-time subscriber (Case read out 35 months in October 2025 and 36 in May 2026), a steady source of 100-, 300- and 1,000-bit cheers, and a regular in Case's opening roll calls and sign-off raps. Their bits have summoned the presidential bit alert about 20 times, and their chat lines get read on the desk, turned into [[segments/ai-rooney|AI Rooney]] complaints, and used against [[characters/crum|Crum]]. They once called in live to [[characters/guy-redacted|Guy Redacted]], and in May 2026 Case made them a VIP. Even after all that, nobody on the show can agree on how to say the name.

## Summoning the President

A 300-bit cheer sets off an alert in Joe Biden's voice: "Listen up, Jack. Skymer just mama-jammed 300 shiny tasty coins, and I've been dying to say this," followed by whatever the president has to say. At 1,000 bits Barack Obama turns up instead: "Skymer just absolutely pounded 1,000 sweet bits straight into the great chasm of progress." Skymer has set these off around 20 times since May 2024:

- [[episodes/2024-07-15|2024-07-15]], six days before the real Biden dropped out: "I'm stepping aside so [[characters/hype-train|Hype Train]] can be our new nominee." ("I graciously accept. I'm now running for president.")
- [[episodes/2025-06-11|2025-06-11]], Case's birthday: Biden: "Happy birthday, young man. I remember when I was your age." Later, Obama: "I'm already in SEAL Team 6 to take out Crum."
- [[episodes/2025-12-18|2025-12-18]]: While Case is struggling to get the Hype Train to appear, Biden turns up to plug "my sweet plaque at the White House." Case: "Thank you so much, Mr. President, for your kind words, and Skymer for summoning the president. Today we are all Skymer."
- [[episodes/2026-03-07|2026-03-07]]: In the middle of Crum's mock trial, Obama arrives on Skymer's 1,000 bits: "Ladies and gentlemen, we got him. Our long national nightmare is over. Fuck Crum."
- [[episodes/2026-07-24|2026-07-24]]: Obama brings back Obamacare to treat Case's ankle injury, though because of budget cuts it's "mostly a thoughts and prayers based treatment." Case: "Skymer, far too kind with the thousand bits that'll go straight into the thoughts and prayers fund for my ankle."

## Shut Up, Crum

Skymer's messages are a reliable source of material against Crum, and [[characters/munch|Munch]] reads them out gladly. "Yes, gross Crum. Thank you, Skymer" ([[episodes/2024-03-01|2024-03-01]]). Skymer suggested the insult "J.K. Crum rolling," and Crum himself admitted it was "more fun to say" ([[episodes/2024-08-01|2024-08-01]]). Munch thanked Skymer for apologizing "for anyone having to see Crum" ([[episodes/2025-02-12|2025-02-12]]), and in a debate over baby photos agreed with their theory that Crum "was in a cocoon from most of his childhood" ("That sounds right, Skymer," [[episodes/2025-12-09|2025-12-09]]). While Crum was supposed to be awaiting his dick punch, chat, "and particularly Skymer," wanted to know: "Why aren't you dead yet, Crum?" ([[episodes/2026-03-19|2026-03-19]], see [[storylines/crum-dick-punch|Crum Dick Punch]]). More recently: "Point is, shut up, Crum. Skymer gets it" ([[episodes/2026-07-13|2026-07-13]]); "Yes, absolutely, Skymer, give him Havana syndrome" ([[episodes/2026-09-01|2026-09-01]]); and at skeleton Munch's Rennerton press conference, a "great point, Skymer" about how to stop future Crum attacks: "the answer is 100% execute Crum" ([[episodes/2026-11-07|2026-11-07]]).
