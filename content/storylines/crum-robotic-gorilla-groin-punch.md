# Crum's Robotic Gorilla Groin Punch Bet

**Crum's Robotic Gorilla Groin Punch Bet** is the active narrative arc stemming from the [[munch-crum-rivalry|Munch–Crum rivalry]]. Due to a crippling gambling addiction, [[../characters/crum|Crum]] lost a high-stakes bet and is scheduled to be punched squarely in the groin by a robotic gorilla on an upcoming Monday broadcast.

## Status

**Active / Imminent.** Slated for execution on Monday.

## Lore & Premise

- **The Gambling Addiction:** Crum suffers from an uncontrolled, crippling gambling addiction that frequently lands him in absurd and dangerous wagers.
- **The Consequence:** Having lost this latest bet, the agreed-upon penalty is a full-force punch to the groin delivered by a mechanical / robotic gorilla fist.
- **Munch's Role:** [[../characters/munch|Munch (Ralph Munchcut)]] actively relishes Crum's impending doom, taking community suggestions on Discord for why Crum deserves the punishment and gleefully reminding him of the date.
- **Crum's Defense & Training:** Rather than backing out, Crum insists he has been rigorous in physical preparation, boasting of "super strength in spits," crushing an electric toothbrush to dust as proof of his resilience, and issuing warnings like: *"My penis can handle a lot... good luck to that gorilla's fist."*
