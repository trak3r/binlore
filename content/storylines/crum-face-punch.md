# Crum Face Punch

**Crum Face Punch** (also called the **Crumsplosion**) is Part 1 of the Crum Punch trilogy on *Barely Informed News*. Debate adversary [[../characters/munch|Ralph Munchcut]] weaponizes [[../characters/crum|Leonard Crum]]'s gambling addiction, goading him into borrowing a catastrophic sum from the silent Rock-afire animatronic gorilla. When Crum cannot repay, Munch talks him into accepting a single full-force punch to the face as settlement.

After weeks of on-air debt tease and countdown graphics, the arc climaxes on **June 17, 2025** when the mechanical gorilla punches Crum and his head completely explodes on live television.

## Status

**Resolved / Concluded.** Crum's head is destroyed on the June 17, 2025 "Gorilla Punch Day" telecast. Aftermath and corpse-denial continue in [[join-the-wall|Join the Wall]] (Part 2); the later groin-punch sequel is [[crum-dick-punch|Crum Dick Punch]] (Part 3).

## Background

- **The Rock-afire Creditor:** The lender is a keyboard-playing Rock-afire animatronic gorilla—an on-set prop with no dialogue, only animation. Crum's debt is framed as a binding gambling liability owed to the machine itself.
- **Munch's Campaign:** Munch agitates for execution, refuses to update favorable graphics, and rallies chat toward Crum's death as the debt "wins" tick down.
- **Escalating Stakes:** Early broadcasts peg the debt near $100k–$115k with per-debate payouts (~$15k/win) and an eight-debate clock. A face punch (sometimes confused on air with a groin strike) becomes the forfeit if Crum loses or fails to clear the balance.
- **Match Point:** By mid-June the debt is nearly cleared and Crum sits one loss from the pneumatic strike.

## The Climax: Crumsplosion (2025-06-17)

On [[../episodes/2025-06-17|Quick News and Crum Punch]], the network executes "Gorilla Punch Day." The mechanical gorilla lands the blow; Crum's head explodes on air. Munch freezes in post-purpose catatonia—described as glitched, hibernating because his central purpose has been fulfilled—while the rest of the broadcast processes the kill through surreal aftermath segments.
