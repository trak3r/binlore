# No Breaks November

**No Breaks November** is Barely Informed News' month-long daily broadcast commitment for November 2026: a telecast every Monday through Saturday, with Sundays off for mental health. [[../characters/case-blackwell|Case]] launched it on the first broadcast of the month to drive follows for the *13 Freak Street* horror-comedy anthology Kickstarter, with a special broadcast planned for the November 20 Kickstarter launch.

The commitment quickly became a running bit. Case slams a "No Breaks November" graphic over nearly every story, [[../characters/crum|Crum]] and the desk mock the catchphrase, and [[../characters/cryptozeus|Brandon]] publicly refuses to sign on to it.

## Status

**Active.** Daily broadcasts continue through November 2026. The arc concludes at the end of the month; the November 20 Kickstarter launch special is the planned centerpiece.

## Background & Format

- **Schedule:** Monday–Saturday broadcasts around 4 PM, Sundays off.
- **Purpose:** Case calls the Kickstarter "ostensibly the reason for No Breaks November." Every broadcast plugs the follow count: 103 at launch, 119 by 2026-11-06, 157 by 2026-11-10.
- **The graphic:** a "No Breaks November" slam graphic that Case hits so often the studio comments on it. On 2026-11-07 he declares it fits every story, and that "there's only the No Breaks November graphic."
- **Catchphrase escalation:** Case insists the phrase is "a movement" and later that "No Breaks November means love."
- **Holdout:** Brandon declares he doesn't subscribe to No Breaks November because he's a streamer.
