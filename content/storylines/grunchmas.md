# Grunchmas

**Grunchmas** is Barely Informed News' annual holiday special in which [[../characters/case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]] embodies **The Grunch**—a horny green Grinch variant—and adjudicates the community-submitted **Fucks List** (who fucks / who doesn't) from a mountaintop scrying cave. The ritual returns each December with green face paint, psychic letter portals, speed-run rap verdicts, and the Grunch's claimed yearly death/hibernation/sweat-cocoon cycle.

By [[../episodes/2025-12-19|Grunchmas 2025]] the network billed the fucks-list rite as the **fourth annual** edition, confirming a multi-year tradition rather than a one-off bit.

## Status

**Active / Recurring Annual.** Documented specials in 2023, 2024, and 2025; expected to return each December unless cancelled on air.

## Background & Ritual

- **The Grunch:** Case in green (and occasionally pink) paint; emerges from an amniotic sack / sweat cocoon for a few days of orgiastic adjudication, then "dies" or re-cocoons until next year. Functionally illiterate—reads viewer letters by vibing psychic wavelengths.
- **The Fucks List:** Discord/community submissions of celebrities, memes, network staff, and fictional entities; Grunch rules Fuck / Doesn't Fuck with absurdist rationale, often finishing in a freestyle rap speed-run.
- **Side Bits:** Crowdsourced "Night Before Crumsmas" poetry, How To with Jeb holiday tutorials, Hype Train "Fucksmas" derailments, and nearly mandatory Munch & Crum holiday interruptions (apologies, naughty-list debates, "does Crum fuck?").
- **Naming Drift:** Archive titles and chat also use Grunch Miss, Grunk-Miss, Grungemas, and Fucksmas; all point at the same annual event.
