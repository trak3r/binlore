# Crum Dick Punch

![Munch Incinerated into a Talking Skeleton during the Crum Dick Punch Climax](https://github.com/trak3r/binlore/releases/download/media-assets/munch-skeleton.jpg)

**Crum Dick Punch** was the premier multi-month investigative storyline and high-stakes wager arc on *Barely Informed News*. Stemming from an astronomical lost gambling wager against a mechanical ape, senior debate analyst [[../characters/crum|Leonard Crum]] faced a binding legal forfeit requiring him to receive a full-force blow to the groin delivered by a pneumatic robotic gorilla on live television.

The storyline reached its cataclysmic climax on **September 7, 2026**, when a catastrophic mechanical failure during the execution triggered a studio inferno that vaporized the Rock-afire Gorilla into ash and incinerated senior debate adversary [[../characters/munch|Ralph Munchcut]] into a living, talking skeleton—leaving Crum completely unscathed.

## Status

**Resolved / Concluded.** The physical liability has been permanently extinguished following the complete destruction and vaporization of the Rock-afire Gorilla on the September 7, 2026 telecast. Munch survives as an immortal talking skeleton.

## Background & Network Record

- **The Gambling Liability:** Crum suffers from a compulsive, chronic gambling addiction. In early 2026, he entered into an astronomical wager with a mechanical gorilla valued at approximately "$200,000-ish." Having lost the bet, the terms legally dictated a full-force blow to his groin from the ape's pneumatic fist on live television.
- **Regulatory Hurdles & Delays:** Throughout the summer of 2026, the execution was repeatedly deferred. Crum cited jurisdictional complications with the Nevada Gaming Commission and Boxing Commission, claiming dick punching was an uncodified 18th-century circus attraction requiring special state licensing. Later delays saw Crum petitioning for iron protective undergarments and seeking a "reverse pardon."
- **Munch's Relentless Campaign:** Debate adversary [[../characters/munch|Ralph Munchcut]] actively agitated for Crum's demise, rallying the chat audience, reciting viewer-submitted dossiers titled *"Reasons You Deserve to Get Your Dick Punched,"* and demanding swift physical retribution.
- **Crum's Defensive Conditioning:** Rather than defaulting, Crum spent weeks undergoing intense physical and spiritual conditioning. He swung 45-pound weighted logs into his crotch, boasted of "super strength in spits," crushed an electric toothbrush to powder on air, and warned: *"My penis can handle a lot... good luck to that gorilla's fist."*

## The Climax: The Labor Day Inferno (2026-09-07)

During the Season 3 finale on September 7, 2026—the final broadcast before anchor [[../characters/case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]] departed on scheduled international leave to Thailand—the network formally executed the wager:

1. **The Debate ("Any Last Words?"):** Munch and Crum faced off at the debate desk. Crum refused to offer last words, insisting his front bottom had achieved peak athleticism through rigorous log conditioning and steel undergarments. Munch demanded the cessation of Crum's voice.
2. **The Execution:** Crum stepped up to the mark to receive the impact from the Rock-afire mechanical gorilla.
3. **The Explosion & Skeleton Transmutation:** As the pneumatic mechanism triggered, a catastrophic malfunction ignited an immediate inferno. The Rock-afire Gorilla was completely reduced to ash. Munch caught the full fiery backblast, which incinerated his flesh and hair, converting him into a living, talking skeleton. Crum emerged entirely unharmed.
4. **The Aftermath:** Refusing defeat, the newly skeletal Munch insisted his transformation was a deliberate demonstration of superior bone structure, declared himself liberated from mortal racial boundaries, and engaged Crum in a debate over Crayola versus RoseArt crayons.
