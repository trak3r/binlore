# Rennerton

**Rennerton** (also spelled **Renerton**) is the network's municipal simulator: a chat-elected mayoral city that [[../characters/case-blackwell|Case Blackwell]] plays live as whichever character wins the ballot. Early lore ties the city to SimCity-style planning; the October 2024 *Return To Renerton* marathon weekend ran city-builder sessions (including **They Are Billions** and a **Cities: Skylines** aftermath reference) across successive administrations that routinely end in tornadoes, Godzilla, nuclear fire, or zombie "extreme loiterers."

Case later traced the network's own origins to a SimCity stream that preceded the news-desk format—making Rennerton both a recurring bit and a callback to BIN's founding city-planning era.

## Status

**Dormant / Lore Callbacks.** The flagship mayoral marathon cluster lands in October 2024; later broadcasts still treat Rennerton as an established zombie municipality (e.g. Munch & Crum platform debates) without a full return stream.

## Background & Mechanics

- **Chat Elects, Case Plays:** Flash polls and town halls fill the ballot with network personalities (and occasional objects). Case then pilots the city-builder *in character* as that mayor—impressions, policy raps, and press conferences included.
- **Spellings:** On-air and archive titles oscillate between Renerton and Rennerton; both refer to the same city.
- **Games:** Pre-marathon chatter frames the city as a SimCity project; the documented October 13 marathon identifies **They Are Billions** as the mayoring software on the afternoon continuation, while the next weekday show cites a weekend **Cities: Skylines** session that ended in zombie apocalypse.
- **Recurring Mayors & Lore:** Pepito, Jeb Nogget, Crum, Munch, Peter Gibbon, Hot Dog Sandwich, Xena Warrior Princess, Godzilla, Gina Darling's forehead, and a Hamtaro-loving hamster have all held (or contested) office. Civic brands include Renerton Gold beer and river burials.

## Flagship Weekend (October 2024)

Two *Return To Renerton* telecasts aired on **2024-10-13**, followed by a Halloween election-night sequel:

1. **Morning/afternoon marathon** (`DRCre_ofu50`, ~4h37): Pepito opens as canine strongman; Kasem G, Crum, Chet, and Peter Gibbon cycle through administrations across ~134 simulated years before a noon handoff.
2. **Continuation** ([[../episodes/2024-10-13|sugAmJtb3HU]], ~2h41): Pepito hits 600k citizens via hospital bulldozing; Jeb's term collapses into tornado/Godzilla/nuclear cascade; a hot dog sandwich wins unanimously; Munch rebuilds from ten survivors; Xena's term ends in a misclicked civilian slaughter.
3. **Halloween sequel** ([[../episodes/2024-10-31|2024-10-31]]): Godzilla, Gina Darling's forehead, a hamster, Munch under "Munchcut law," and finally Crum (69–0 landslide) attempt to fortify the ruined city against flesh-eating loiterers.
