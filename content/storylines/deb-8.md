# Deb-8

**Deb-8** is Barely Informed News' eight-hour debate marathon: a stretch-goal special that packs the full desk roster into a single continuous telecast of stacked debate formats. Announced in June 2024, funded by a birthday-stream sub goal, previewed the night before, and executed on **July 20, 2024** as *Deb-8 - Eight Hours of DEBATE* (~8h12m).

The event is the network's clearest "debate virus" showcase—Munch & Crum blocks, Jambalaya identity games, Trip coin-flip street verdicts, Jo Rogen AI duels, and guest couch takeovers—rather than a slow-burn rivalry arc.

## Status

**Resolved / Concluded.** The flagship marathon aired July 20, 2024. Later broadcasts occasionally tease another long debate/Crum stream, but no second Deb-8 has been logged.

## Background & Format

- **Stretch Goal:** Case sets an eight-hour debate marathon goal for July during early-June planning, aiming to vary formats beyond a single Munch & Crum block (town halls, guests, specialty desks).
- **Goal Met:** On Case's birthday Elden Ring stream ([[../episodes/2024-06-11|2024-06-11]]), a 20-gift sub bomb clears the marathon goal.
- **Eve Preview:** [[../episodes/2024-07-19|2024-07-19]] locks the window (roughly 11am–7pm) and tees up the next day's slate.
- **Execution:** Twelve-ish segments across eight hours; debuts Jambalaya "secret identity" format; ends by raiding out and transmitting the "debate virus."

## The Marathon (2024-07-20)

On [[../episodes/2024-07-20|Deb-8]], highlights include:

- **Jambalaya debut** — secret Obama voter / cannibal / bedwetter panels with Chet, Liliana, Grandma Crumble Bottom, Tyler, plus guests Bill and Tim.
- **Four+ Munch & Crum blocks** — wiki feet, spouse verification, sweet vs salty, Alien vs Predator, dental status symbols, orphan gastronomy, spoon vs knife, Spike vs Luffy, Country Jamboree rematch; finale debates 8-hour vs 24-hour streams.
- **Specialty desks** — DJ Newsic vs Country Bear; double AI Jo Rogen; Trip Bradstein coin-flip street debates; How To with Jeb (Harvard RISA framework + scat lesson); Trent at the Movies; Amongst the Web; Conspiracy Time with Guy Redacted.
