# The Scrotum Merger

**The Scrotum Merger** is a supernatural body-horror storyline initiated on the [[../episodes/2026-09-04|2026-09-04 broadcast]] of *Barely Informed News*. When a planned [[../segments/munch-and-crum|Munch & Crum]] debate was disrupted, chat summoned the sleazy mystic sorcerer [[../characters/cremus-tremando|Cremus Tremando]] to cast a "Freaky Friday" body-swap spell on [[../characters/crum|Crum]] and [[../characters/peter-gibbon|Peter Gibbon]].

The invocation of *"Bielzotroth, the God of Mistriff"* backfired catastrophically, fusing Peter Gibbon and Crum into a chimeric, two-minded entity christened **[[../characters/scrotum|Scrotum]]**.

## Status

**Active / Unresolved.** As of 2026-09-04, Cremus Tremando disappeared via "snake powers" declaring "no refunds," leaving Peter Gibbon and Crum physically and mentally merged.

## Lore & Mechanics

- **The Failed Ritual:** Cremus Tremando charged a fee ("mail me the bill") and cast the body-swap incantation. Instead of swapping vessels, the spell compressed both consciousnesses and physical forms together.
- **Dual Consciousness:** Peter Gibbon retains primary physical control of the vocal cords and boasts of his deadlifting leg strength, but experiences psychic bleed-through from Crum's frantic memories of stealing sliced ham at grocery stores.
- **Host Duties:** Unperturbed by the horrific metaphysical calamity, Scrotum anchored the news desk and hosted the [[../segments/amongst-the-web|Amongst the Web]] segment.
