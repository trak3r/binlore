# The Scrotum Merger

**The Scrotum Merger** is a catastrophic metaphysical incident occurring during the [[../episodes/2026-09-04|2026-09-04 broadcast]] of *Barely Informed News*. Retained by the network to conduct an emergency supernatural intervention, freelance mystic consultant [[../characters/cremus-tremando|Cremus Tremando]] attempted to cast an ancient body-swapping incantation across the studio. The ritual misfired catastrophically, entangling the broadcast signals, physical forms, and consciousness of senior debate analyst [[../characters/crum|Crum]] and exiled former producer [[../characters/peter-gibbon|Peter Gibbon]] into a volatile amalgamated entity known around the studio as "Scrotum."

When a planned [[../segments/munch-and-crum|Munch & Crum]] debate was disrupted by supernatural instability, Cremus Tremando attempted a "Freaky Friday" translocation invoking *"Bielzotroth, the God of Mistriff."* Instead of executing a clean transfer, the spell fused Peter Gibbon and Crum's presence on air.

## Status

**Active / Broadcast Anomaly.** Tremando abruptly fled the studio via "snake powers" shouting "no refunds." Newsroom staff and technical crew were forced to broadcast with the fused identity for the remainder of the telecast.

## Incident Log & Manifestations

- **The Failed Ritual:** Tremando demanded a bill be sent to his estate and cast the incantation, immediately fleeing when the newsroom monitors began flashing uncontrollably.
- **Dual Consciousness Phenomenon:** Peter Gibbon reported severe psychic bleed-through from Crum's frantic memories of shoplifting sliced deli ham at local supermarkets, interspersed with boasts about his own deadlifting capabilities.
- **Broadcast Hijacking:** Despite the psychic disorientation, the amalgamated presence proceeded to occupy the primary news desk, read breaking international dispatches, and host the [[../segments/amongst-the-web|Amongst the Web]] interactive video desk.
