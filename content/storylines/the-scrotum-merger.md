# The Scrotum Merger

**The Scrotum Merger** is an improvised body-swap comedic arc on the [[../episodes/2026-09-04|2026-09-04 broadcast]] of *Barely Informed News*. Born out of a live fumble with character switching where Case accidentally muddied the waters between [[../characters/crum|Crum]] and [[../characters/peter-gibbon|Peter Gibbon]], Case played off the confusion as an in-universe disaster caused by the sleazy mystic sorcerer [[../characters/cremus-tremando|Cremus Tremando]].

When a planned [[../segments/munch-and-crum|Munch & Crum]] debate was disrupted, Cremus Tremando attempted to cast a "Freaky Friday" body-swap spell invoking *"Bielzotroth, the God of Mistriff."* Instead of swapping bodies, the spell supposedly fused Peter Gibbon and Crum together, jokingly dubbed "Scrotum."

## Status

**Active / Gag.** Cremus Tremando disappeared via "snake powers" declaring "no refunds." Case played with the fused identity for the remainder of the broadcast before moving on.

## Lore & Mechanics

- **The Failed Ritual:** Cremus Tremando demanded a bill be sent to him and cast the incantation, immediately fleeing when things went wrong.
- **Dual Consciousness Bit:** Peter Gibbon claimed to experience psychic bleed-through from Crum's frantic memories of stealing sliced ham at grocery stores while boasting about his own leg strength.
- **Host Duties:** Peter Gibbon continued anchoring the news desk and hosting the [[../segments/amongst-the-web|Amongst the Web]] segment.
